- (decision) Batch: Role Playing, First Principles Thinking, Five Whys, Question Storming (in order)
- (technique) started Role Playing
- (idea by coach) Warehouse picker: zero-typing capture - one scan + one tap per movement, else staff fall back to paper
- (idea by coach) Blame-proof attribution: audit trail that protects honest workers (shift/handover evidence), not just catches them
- (insight by user) Inventory manager story: system showed 60, only 32 truly available (damaged + already reserved) - promised 50 to customer, had to explain to customer and boss
- (idea by user) Show what is ACTUALLY AVAILABLE, not just recorded on-hand: on-hand minus damaged minus reserved
- (idea by user) Damaged stock and reserved stock must be distinct statuses excluded from sellable quantity
- (idea by coach) Stock ladder per product: on hand -> damaged -> reserved -> promisable, never a single bare number
- (idea by coach) Commit-time gap warning: when promising qty > promisable, show incoming POs and allow split promise or reserve incoming stock
- (insight by user) Damage discovery flow today: worker finds crushed box while picking, sets aside, tells supervisor verbally; nobody updates system (busy); damaged stays available; later nobody remembers who/when
- (insight by user) Root gap: damage information lives in conversation and a pile on the floor, not in the system - verbal handoff loses who/when
- (idea by coach) 'Flag it' on picker scanner: scan + 1 photo moves unit to quarantine (blocked, not written off), auto-captures who/when/where, replaces verbal tell
- (insight by coach) Real job: make reporting damage easier than telling the supervisor
- (idea by user) Supervisor damage card shows photo, product, location, quantity at a glance for fast decisions
- (idea by user) No per-item notifications (alert fatigue) - one prioritized issues list with urgent items highlighted
- (idea by user) One-tap approve/reject of damage reports from the issues list
- (idea by coach) Urgency ranking = money at risk x customer commitment (damaged committed stock flashes top; slow stock waits)
- (idea by coach) Batch similar damage reports (same product/aisle/hour) into one card - pattern hints root cause e.g. forklift
- (insight by coach) Patterns in damage reports are themselves a signal
- (insight by user) Context signal: owner thinks in taka - Bangladesh market, BDT currency (confirm as target market assumption)
- (idea by user) Owner: no end-of-month surprise - surface money lost to damaged, missing, expired stock as it happens
- (idea by user) Owner: show where money is tied up (capital locked in stock)
- (idea by user) Owner: identify which products are losing money
- (idea by user) Owner: trust the numbers without visiting warehouse daily - remote confidence in data accuracy
- (idea by coach) Trust Score % on owner home with reasons for drops (uncounted aisles, pending damage, reasonless adjustments)
- (idea by coach) Leakage meter in taka: monthly loss split damaged / expired / unexplained
- (idea by user) Drill-down from unexplained loss to every discrepancy -> transaction, responsible user, timestamp
- (idea by user) Investigate and assign discrepancy for review (case workflow) instead of just showing a scary number
- (decision by user) Rejected arbitrary Trust Score - owner trusts evidence: verified stock counts and resolved discrepancies
- (insight by user) Every number on the owner view must be actionable and traceable to evidence, not a vanity metric
- (idea by coach) Owner dashboard = list of open discrepancy cases (amount, product, location, delta, assignee, age) instead of charts
- (idea by coach) Close cases with explanation category (theft, receiving error, picking error, unrecorded damage) - closed cases reveal where the business leaks
- (insight by user) Fraud vector: receive fewer than PO, mark full PO as received, rely on nobody checking actual quantity - shortage hidden at receiving, not in stock records
- (idea by user) Segregation of duties: receiver must not be able to approve their own receiving record
- (idea by coach) Blind receiving: receiver never sees PO quantity, must count and enter actual
- (idea by coach) Supplier invoice/challan vs received mismatch flagged before payment - finance never pays for phantom units
- (idea by coach) Random unpredictable spot re-count (~1 in 20 deliveries) by second person before stock goes available
- (insight by user) Fraud vector 2: transfers - dispatch 50 recorded, only 40 arrive; if receiving warehouse confirms without counting, 10 vanish between locations
- (idea by user) Transfers must require a real count at the receiving location before confirmation (no blind confirm)
- (insight by coach) Weak point is always a handoff where one side trusts the other's number (supplier->receiver, WH A->WH B, worker->supervisor)
- (idea by coach) In-transit as a real location with a responsible owner (truck/driver); missing units stay attributed and auto-become a case after 48h
- (idea by coach) Two-key blind transfers: dispatcher and receiver count independently, system compares, mismatch opens case
- (idea by coach) Sealed transfer: seal/QR number recorded at dispatch, verified at arrival
- (decision by user) Controls are automatic and risk-based with configurable settings - not one strict/simple mode for all
- (idea by user) Risk triggers for stricter checks: high-value products, repeated discrepancies, suspicious transfers
- (idea by user) Low-risk everyday movements stay simple (no extra friction)
- (idea by user) Businesses can tune risk rules to their size and workflow
- (idea by coach) Earned/decaying risk levels: product/location/person escalates to strict after repeated discrepancies, relaxes after clean counts - honest teams feel the system get lighter
- (question by coach) Open: is risk-scoring individual people acceptable (privacy, morale, labor norms)?
- (idea by user) Sales screen: available-to-promise quantity, split by warehouse
- (idea by user) Sales screen: when incoming stock arrives (expected dates from POs/transfers)
- (idea by user) Give sales a trustworthy delivery date, not just a quantity
- (insight by user) Sales fear: promising based on outdated numbers - freshness of availability data is a core need
- (idea by coach) Freshness stamp on every availability number: last verified by count X days ago, N movements since
- (idea by coach) Incoming date confidence from supplier on-time history (PO says Thursday, realistic Saturday)
- (idea by coach) Soft hold: rep reserves qty for 30 min during a customer call so others can't promise the same units
- (insight by user) Accountant month-end pain: changing purchase costs, damaged stock not recorded properly, inventory value doesn't match general ledger
- (idea by user) Clear, explicit valuation method required before sign-off
- (idea by user) Transaction-level audit trail backing every value figure
- (idea by user) Reconciliation report: physical stock value vs general ledger accounts
- (idea by coach) Every stock movement carries its cost at the moment it happens - valuation is a sum of movements, never a recalculation
- (idea by coach) Damage/write-off approval posts value impact automatically (journal-ready entry) so GL and stock never drift
- (idea by coach) Month-end lock: close a period so back-dated stock edits can't silently change a signed-off valuation
- (technique) started First Principles Thinking (Role Playing done: manager, picker, supervisor, owner, dishonest employee x2, sales, accountant)
- (insight by coach) Truth 1: stock is physical; the system is only a claim about it - best it can be is recently verified
- (insight by user) Truth 2: every movement changes reality whether recorded or not; each unrecorded receive/move/damage/sale widens the gap between physical and recorded stock
- (insight by coach) Accuracy = verification minus unrecorded movements; only two levers produce accuracy: capture and counting - everything else consumes it
- (idea by coach) Drift rate per location per week (gap revealed at each count) as the core product health metric
- (idea by coach) Recording as a side effect of work already done (scan-to-pick), never a separate task
- (insight by user) Truth 3: every unit always has a location and a state (shelf, in transit, reserved, damaged), whether or not anyone knows it
- (idea by coach) Core model: one ledger of moves between (location, state) cells; receive/transfer/damage/reserve/sell are named moves; stock ladder = cells summed by state
- (idea by coach) 'Unknown/unlocated' cell: count shortages move there with value instead of being deleted, until explained
- (insight by user) Model gap: cost changes involve no physical movement; carton->pieces and kit assembly change identity/quantity
- (idea by user) Model must support non-movement adjustments (e.g. revaluation) as first-class ledger entries
- (idea by user) Model must support transformations (unit conversion, kitting/assembly) consuming inputs and producing outputs
- (idea by user) Transformations and adjustments preserve full history and value lineage of original stock
- (idea by coach) Three ledger entry kinds: Move (location/state), Transform (inputs->outputs with cost lineage, e.g. piece inherits 1/24 carton cost), Revalue (value only) - all append-only
- (idea by coach) Reverse lineage: trace kit/piece back to source batch and assembler for complaints, recalls, expiry
- (insight by user) Truth 4: people prioritize physical work over records under time pressure; extra recording effort gets delayed or skipped
- (decision by user) System must capture inventory changes as naturally and quickly as possible inside existing workflows
- (idea by coach) Recording effort budget: routine low-risk floor actions <= 1 scan + 1 tap (~2s); features breaking it don't ship
- (idea by coach) Offline-first capture: scans queue on device and sync later - critical given Bangladesh connectivity/power cuts
- (idea by coach) Context defaults pre-fill reason/location/qty from pick list, PO, last action; users only correct exceptions
- (technique) started Five Whys (problem: system showed 60, only 32 available)
- (insight by coach) Why1: damaged and reserved stock not reflected in available number
- (insight by user) Why2: damage reporting and reservations run through separate, often manual workflows; staff don't update the inventory system immediately
- (insight by user) Why3: system too cumbersome for quick daily tasks, or lacks a simple way for sales and warehouse staff to record reservations and damage in one place
- (idea by user) One simple shared system where sales and warehouse staff both record reservations and damage quickly
- (insight by coach) Why4 hypothesis: systems designed around back-office buyer (forms, approvals) then pushed to floor/counter where people have 5s, one hand, a phone
- (insight by user) Why4 (agreed): existing systems prioritize managers' reporting over workers' daily tasks; recording feels like extra work instead of part of the job
- (decision by user) Make essential actions simple for every role, without unnecessary forms or approvals
- (insight by user) Why5 ROOT: recording is extra work because recorders spend effort but get little immediate benefit - value flows up to managers, never down
- (decision by user) Design principle: every record must give the recorder something back immediately
- (idea by user) Worker payoff: scanning shows the next picking task (guided work)
- (idea by user) Worker payoff: instant confirmation their work is recorded
- (idea by user) Worker payoff: protection from blame for discrepancies they didn't cause (their records prove it)
- (idea by coach) Sales payoff: reservation protects the rep's deal from colleagues; supervisor payoff: approval clears inbox
- (idea by coach) 'My record' view per worker: daily picks, discrepancies, confirmed flags - record becomes alibi and reputation

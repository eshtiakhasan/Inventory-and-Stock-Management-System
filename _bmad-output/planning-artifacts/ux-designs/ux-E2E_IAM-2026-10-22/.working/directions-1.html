১ · Big & Bold Floor বড় ও স্পষ্ট

Built for a gloved thumb under a tin roof: every screen does one job, the quantity to pick is the biggest thing on it, and the Scan button is a 72px full-width bar you can hit without looking. Heavy 800-900 weight type and thick borders survive glare and cheap screens; the trade-off is scrolling on longer orders and an owner view that shows fewer numbers, only the ones that need action.

৩ · Compact Data-first ঘন ও তথ্যভিত্তিক

For the experienced storekeeper and the owner who wants numbers, not pictures: a table-style pick list with tabular figures, a summary strip on top, and Scan + Flag side-by-side in one 52px row. Everything for an order fits above the fold, even a 12-line order, while Bangla stays at 16px and rows stay 56px tall; the cost is a steeper first day for new or low-literacy staff and smaller secondary labels.

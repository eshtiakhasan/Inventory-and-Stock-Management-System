### Summary

The decomposition is sound and passes the catalogue well-formedness rules. Moving Warehouse to IdentityAccess (ADR-006) is justified and was clearly surfaced. The two points to weigh before approving are the unspecified authorization on StockLedger reads and the unclear scope of the once-only action ID for multi-step requests, and WebApp should be called out as an addition beyond the confirmed eight blocks.

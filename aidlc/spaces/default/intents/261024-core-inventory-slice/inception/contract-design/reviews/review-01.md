### Summary

The framework choices (REST with OpenAPI, session cookie plus CSRF, one error shape, versioning, full-response idempotency) are sound and trace to Q1-Q6. The contract is not yet implementable without guessing. Most endpoint bodies and responses are undefined, several module interfaces that components.md depends on are missing, the authorization matrix for reads is absent, and the idempotency design leaves the concurrent same-key case undefined. The authorization claim to close the earlier stock-read point is asserted rather than specified.

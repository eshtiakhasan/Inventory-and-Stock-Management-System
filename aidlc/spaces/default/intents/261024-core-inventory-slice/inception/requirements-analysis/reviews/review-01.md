### Summary

The requirements are well traced and mostly testable, with good Given/When/Then coverage of stock rules and blind receiving. Four Major gaps would force engineering to come back with questions: role permissions with no screen, stuck partly received orders, cancelling a part-picked dispatch order, and live sessions of deactivated users. Fixing those four brings it to READY.

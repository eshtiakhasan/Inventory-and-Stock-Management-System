> R-01 Reviewer note: `APP_ORIGIN` is now an explicit allow-list. `security-design.md` (Origin check) splits and trims it, validates each entry at start-up, and matches the `Origin` header exactly against one entry. `Origin: null` and a missing or unparsable header are still refused. The infrastructure specification lists localhost and 127.0.0.1 on ports 8080 and 5173, and traceability NFR1.19 says "matched entry by entry". Both documents now agree.

> R-02 Reviewer note: The init script is gone. The `db` row says "No init scripts". The Environments row describes one mechanism: Vitest global setup creates `mojud_test` if missing, then runs the same migrator, and is safe on any existing volume. Stage 3 and traceability NFR7.2 point at it.

> R-03 Reviewer note: Verify uses the separate Compose project `-p mojud-verify` in stages 2, 4 and 6 and finishes with `down -v`. The smoke test uses a unique 11-digit phone number and a unique business name. The empty-volume SLI is now exercised on every run.

> R-04 Reviewer note: `db`, `api` and `web` have explicit health check timings, and `web` has a `/healthz` check. The worst-case budget (about 35 s against 60 s) is stated.

> R-05 Reviewer note: Compose v2.24+ is required and checked in preflight. Preflight also checks that ports 8080 and 5433 are free and gives a clear message.

> R-06 Reviewer note: Teardown runs in `finally` and from the SIGINT and SIGTERM handlers. Teardown is skipped when preflight fails, because it applies only once stage 2 has started. `npm run up` leaves the app running after the merge.

> R-07 Reviewer note: The smoke test uses 127.0.0.1:8080, and that origin is in the allow-list.

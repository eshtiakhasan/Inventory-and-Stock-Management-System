### Summary

The design is largely complete and consistent with the contract, but three Major findings block it: the frontend's blanket "any 401 means session ended" rule contradicts the sign-in and app-load flows, SESSION_ENDED and NOT_SIGNED_IN are assigned to the same condition in different places, and AC1.1.4 is marked covered although the unit has no endpoint on which to prove it. Fix those three and tidy the Minor items to reach READY.

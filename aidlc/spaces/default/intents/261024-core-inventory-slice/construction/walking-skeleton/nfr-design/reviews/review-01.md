### Summary

The NFR design covers every requirement, but six Major defects block it. The clean-up advisory lock is not tied to a connection, the `db` health check can pass before the database is ready on a fresh volume, the cookie name contradicts contract C1, the `authenticate` query and its order are contradictory and extend sessions before CSRF and active checks, and sign-in can still return 400 on malformed JSON. The nginx header design can also drop the CSP from `index.html`. Fix these and re-review.

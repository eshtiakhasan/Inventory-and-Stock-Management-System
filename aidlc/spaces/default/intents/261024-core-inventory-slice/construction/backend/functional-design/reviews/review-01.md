### Summary

The stock rules are mostly sound, and the receiving flow has no blind-view leak: only the dedicated ReceivingView type is reachable by workers. But the dispatch-line cancel result contradicts the database invariant, which would make every cancel fail. BR7.3 also needs cross-module queries that the component graph and C5 do not allow, and the idempotency and lock-row details cannot be implemented as written.

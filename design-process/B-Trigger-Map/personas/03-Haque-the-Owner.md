# Mr. Haque the Owner: Secondary Persona

> Secondary target. Decides whether the business adopts Mojud and keeps using it.

**Priority:** SECONDARY
**Role in flywheel:** sets the business up, sees explained losses, assigns cases → keeps the distributor on Mojud (product proof)
**Created:** 2026-10-23
**Sources:** PRD §2.1 JTBD, UJ-6, UJ-8; brief → Who This Serves. Fears and quotes are `[ASSUMPTION]` until interviews.

## Profile Summary

**Mr. Haque owns a mid-sized FMCG distributorship with two warehouses and about
twenty staff. He built it on relationships and a khata, and now runs it mostly
by phone.** He doesn't want reports; he wants to *trust* the numbers without
driving to Gazipur, and to know where taka is leaking while there's still time
to act. He'll judge Mojud by whether his staff actually use it and whether its
figures hold up when he asks "why?".

## Context of Use

- **Where:** home, office, car; rarely on the floor
- **Device:** a mid-range smartphone, sometimes a desktop at the office
- **Moments:** setting the business up (UJ-8), a weekly or monthly look at losses, following an unexplained figure to its case (UJ-6), assigning a case to the manager

## Goals (usage context)

- See this month's losses in taka by reason, at a glance
- Follow any figure down to the transaction, person and time
- Assign a problem to someone and know it will be closed
- Get set up without a consultant (Excel import, warehouses, users)

## Driving Forces

**Positive (wants):**
- ✅ Control from a distance
- ✅ Evidence he can act on, not scores
- ✅ A professional tool he's proud to hand to staff

**Negative (fears):**
- ❌ Discovering losses at month end, too late
- ❌ Being kept in the dark or lied to
- ❌ Investing time in a tool his staff ignore
- ❌ His stock and money data being lost or exposed

## Illustrative Quote

> "Don't tell me stock is short. Tell me which carton, which day, and who counted."
> *(illustrative, not from an interview)*

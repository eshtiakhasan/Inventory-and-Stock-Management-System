## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let any User get into Mojud with a phone number and password, in the language they read, and land as themselves, so every action on a shared phone is recorded under the right name (FR-2, SM-3).

**User Situation:** Karim, first morning after Mr. Haque set Mojud up. He holds the shared warehouse phone and a slip of paper with his phone number and temporary password. Hope: get in quickly and see his tasks in Bangla. Worry: recording under someone else's name, or getting stuck on an English screen.

**Success Criteria:**
- Karim can switch the screen to Bangla before typing anything, with one tap.
- He signs in with his phone number and temporary password, and is taken straight to set his own password (10.2) before he sees any task (FR-54).
- A wrong attempt says "Sign-in details are not correct." without naming the field (FR-2); after 5 failed attempts he reads a plain pause message with no ticking countdown.
- Nothing on the screen is pre-filled with the previous person's phone number.
- On a phone marked **Shared phone** (asked once, at the first sign-in on the device; on by default for floor Roles), the browser is asked not to save the password and a short hint tells him not to save it (DD-8).

**Entry Points:**
- Opening the Mojud link (sent by the Owner) in Chrome.
- Opening the installed Mojud icon (PWA, FR-40) while nobody is signed in on this phone.
- After Sign out from More or [10.3 Settings: Me](../10.3-settings-me/10.3-settings-me.md).
- Any deep link while signed out (after sign-in, the User goes to that link if their Role may open it).
- "Back to sign in" from [11.1 Owner recovery](../../11-haque-recovers-his-account/11.1-owner-recovery/11.1-owner-recovery.md).

**Exit Points:**
- First sign-in on this device → the **Shared phone** question (`signin-shared-question`, DD-8), then the exits below.
- Signed in with a temporary password → [10.2 Change temporary password](../10.2-change-temporary-password/10.2-change-temporary-password.md) (always, before anything else; FR-54).
- Signed in with own password → the Role home: My tasks ([01.1](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md)) for Warehouse worker; Issues inbox for Supervisor and Manager; Product search for Sales rep; Owner view ([04.1](../../04-haque-traces-an-unexplained-loss/04.1-owner-view/04.1-owner-view.md)) for Owner and Accountant (IA → Home and navigation per Role).
- "Owner? Use your recovery code" → [11.1 Owner recovery](../../11-haque-recovers-his-account/11.1-owner-recovery/11.1-owner-recovery.md).
- "Create a business" → [09.1 Sign up](../../09-haque-sets-up-his-business/09.1-sign-up/09.1-sign-up.md) (see Open Question 9).

## Language before sign-in (decision log)

EXPERIENCE.md requires that the language before sign-in is decided and logged. Decision for this spec:

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|------|--------|
| L1 | The screen opens in the **language last used on this phone** (the last language chosen on any pre-sign-in screen or by the last signed-in User), stored on the device. | New, [ASSUMPTION] |
| L2 | On a phone that has never opened Mojud, it opens in **Bangla**, whatever the browser language. Floor staff read Bangla first; a cheap Android set to English must not trap Karim on an English screen (scenario 10 worry). | New, [ASSUMPTION] |
| L3 | A **Language switch** ("বাংলা \| English") sits at the top of the form; one tap re-renders the whole screen without reload and without clearing typed fields (EXPERIENCE.md → Language switch: "S27, and on S2 before sign-in"). | EXPERIENCE.md |
| L4 | On a User's **first** sign-in, the language on screen at that moment becomes that User's saved language (FR-45). On later sign-ins, the User's saved language takes over after sign-in, even if the sign-in screen showed the other one. | New, [ASSUMPTION] |
| L5 | The page sets `lang="bn"` or `lang="en"` to match; each segment of the switch carries its own `lang`. | EXPERIENCE.md → Accessibility Floor |

In scenario 10 this means: the shared phone was last used by Mr. Haque in English, so Karim sees English, taps "বাংলা" first, and signs in; Bangla becomes his saved language. 10.3 then shows Bangla already selected (Open Question 2).

- **Valid:** Sign in enabled; no messages.
- **Invalid (client):** message under the field, `danger` border, `aria-invalid`, focus to the first invalid field; Sign in stays enabled (resubmitting re-validates).

## Technical Notes

- **Shared-phone rules (EXPERIENCE.md → Responsive & Platform, Decided; PRD §9 Q2 open):** never pre-fill the phone number from the last User; never show a "last signed in as" name; sign-out stays one tap away in More; the signed-in name shows in the app bar on floor screens after sign-in.
- **Shared phone setting (DD-8):** stored per device (local storage of the PWA), not per User; values on / off / not set. Not set behaves as on for the form (`autocomplete="off"`, hint) until the first sign-in answers the question [ASSUMPTION]. Sign-up (09.1) sets it off on the Owner's device. Changed in 10.3.
- **Queued actions keep their author (DD-8):** every queued action is stamped at recording time with its author and a signed per-session action token, so it syncs as that author after sign-out and another User on the phone can see only a count of them [ASSUMPTION: signed action tokens to be confirmed in architecture].
- **Pause scope:** the 5-try pause is counted per phone number on the server, not per device, so one locked User does not block the next person on the shared phone. The pause message is returned for that number whether or not an account exists, so it cannot be used to discover which numbers have accounts [ASSUMPTION].
- **Offline sign-in (FR-41, State Patterns):** a User who has signed in online on this phone before may sign in offline; the device keeps only a salted verifier for that User, never the password (NFR-6). A temporary password always needs a connection. Exact mechanism, expiry and local attempt counting are architecture decisions (Open Question 3).
- **Phone normalisation:** store and compare E.164 (+8801XXXXXXXXX); convert Bangla digits (০-৯) to Western digits before validating.
- **Language:** the pre-sign-in language is stored on the device; on first sign-in it is saved to the User (decision L4). After sign-in the User's saved language wins.
- **Routing after success:** temporary password → 10.2 (server flag on the session; every other route redirects to 10.2 until the password is changed); otherwise Role home or the requested deep link if the Role may open it.
- **Security:** HTTPS only (NFR-6); sign-in requests are not cached by the service worker; the page is served from the PWA shell so it opens offline.
- **Live regions:** one polite (online/offline changes) and one assertive (form messages) region exist from page load; text is replaced, not re-created.
- **Reduced motion:** no animation on the switch or messages.

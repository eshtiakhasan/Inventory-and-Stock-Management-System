## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show the signed-in worker every open task in his Assigned Warehouses, with the one to do next at the top, so he never has to ask anyone what comes next (FR-53).

**User Situation:** Karim, at the Narayanganj loading bay, mid-morning. A supplier truck has just backed in with rice and oil; the driver wants a signature and the back of the warehouse has one bar of signal. He opens the installed Mojud app from the home screen of the shared warehouse phone. Hope: get the truck counted fast and be done with it. Worry: that the 8 missing cartons get pinned on him, or that his counts vanish when the network drops.

**Success Criteria:**
- Karim sees his own name in the app bar (shared phone) and the supplier delivery card without scrolling.
- One tap on the Purchase order card opens Receive PO (blind); no expected quantity is visible on the card.
- After any finished task (receipt, dispatch, transfer, count) he lands back here with the next task already on screen (FR-53, UJ-1 step 6).
- Online / offline state and the queue count are always visible (FR-42).

**Entry Points:**
- App launch from the home-screen icon (PWA, FR-40); home for the Warehouse worker role.
- Tasks tab in the bottom tab bar (Warehouse worker and Supervisor).
- Return after finishing a task: Mark delivery complete (01.2), Confirm dispatch (02.1), Finish receipt (05.3), Send Transfer finish (05.2), last count line (08.2).
- Back from a task screen (scroll position kept).

**Exit Points:**
- Purchase order card → [01.2 Receive PO (blind)](../01.2-receive-po/01.2-receive-po.md) (this scenario).
- Dispatch order card → [02.1 Pick Dispatch order](../../02-karim-picks-and-flags-damage/02.1-pick-dispatch-order/02.1-pick-dispatch-order.md).
- Incoming Transfer card → [05.3 Receive Transfer](../../05-karims-transfer-arrives-short/05.3-receive-transfer/05.3-receive-transfer.md).
- Stock count card → [08.2 Count lines](../../08-rafiq-runs-the-weekly-count/08.2-count-lines/08.2-count-lines.md).
- Draft transfer card → [05.2 Send Transfer](../../05-karims-transfer-arrives-short/05.2-send-transfer/05.2-send-transfer.md) with the draft reopened (DD-5).
- Send Transfer header button → [05.2 Send Transfer](../../05-karims-transfer-arrives-short/05.2-send-transfer/05.2-send-transfer.md) (S10 in send mode).
- Offline banner → [01.4 Offline queue](../01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).

**Queued actions keep their author (DD-8):** `{n}` counts only the signed-in User's own queued actions. Actions left on this phone by someone who signed out keep syncing as that person; they are shown only as the count `{m}` ("2 actions from another person waiting to sync"), never by name or content here, and the signed-in User cannot edit, discard or re-send them. When online, the banner shows the `offline.banner.others` text without the "Offline · {n}" part while `{m}` > 0 and they are still syncing [ASSUMPTION].

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Default (online) | Tasks exist, connection up | App bar "Online"; cards with current bar on the first; Scan bar + tab bar | Open a task, Send Transfer, Scan, switch tab |
| Loading | First open or after sync | Last known tasks show at once; skeleton cards in the shape of task cards (`aria-busy="true"` on the list) only when nothing is cached; never a blank screen | Scan bar and tabs usable |
| Empty | No open tasks | "All tasks done for today" + today's summary; Scan bar, Send Transfer and tabs stay | Scan, Send Transfer, More |
| Offline | Connection lost | App bar "Offline" (cloud-off); offline banner with queue count under the app bar; cards from the data on the phone | All task cards still open (FR-41); banner opens 01.4 |
| Not synced | Any task has a queued line | That card's status tag reads "Not synced" (dashed warning, hourglass) | Open task; banner to 01.4 |
| Draft transfer open | The User saved Send Transfer lines without sending (DD-5) | "Draft transfer · not sent" card with destination and line count | Open the draft (send or discard in 05.2) |
| Another person's queue | Actions recorded on this phone by a User who has signed out are still waiting (DD-8) | Banner adds "{m} actions from another person waiting to sync"; their task cards show no "Not synced" tag for this User | None; they sync on their own |
| Syncing | Back online with queued actions | Banner "Syncing {n}…" counting down; tags change back as items clear; banner disappears at 0 | No action needed |
| Offline warning (20 h) | Queue holds actions 20 h old | Banner text changes to the 4-hour warning; announced once | Connect |
| Offline too long (24 h) | Queue reaches 24 h | Banner "Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data…"; recording paused on task screens (never mid-line) | Connect; queue kept |
| Arrival after finishing a task | Returned from 01.2 (or 02.1, 05.2, 05.3, 08.2) | Finished card gone (or "Done"); next task is current; toast "Receipt saved · PO-0311" (or offline toast); focus on the Scan bar | Continue with the next task |

## Technical Notes

- **Data on the phone (offline):** open tasks for the User's Assigned Warehouses with their lines are cached (EXPERIENCE.md → Foundation). Never cache or send ordered, sent or system quantities for blind task types to the client (State Patterns → Blind: "not in the page code").
- **Live regions:** one polite (`role="status"`) and one assertive region exist from page load; toast and online/offline changes go to the polite region.
- **Route focus:** on arrival, focus goes to the `h1`, except after a finished task, where it goes to the Scan bar.
- **Task card markup:** each card is a single `<a>`; the status tag is plain text inside it. No nested buttons.
- **Tasks appear by increment:** only task types for built features show (FR-53).
- **Language:** `lang="bn"` or `lang="en"` on the page per the User; names (Product, Supplier, customer, Warehouse) unmarked and unchanged (FR-45). Western digits in both.
- **Reduced motion:** toast and banner appear without animation; no animation > 200 ms.

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show, in the order they were recorded, every action saved on this phone that has not reached the server yet, and what happened to each one when it synced, so the worker knows for certain that none of his counts were lost (FR-42, FR-44: "No action in the queue is ever lost without a record").

**User Situation:** The signal dropped at the back of the warehouse while Karim was counting. Each Save showed "Saved on phone, will sync" and the lines carried "Not synced" chips. After Mark delivery complete he is on My tasks; the orange banner says "Offline · 4 actions waiting". He walks toward the roller door, taps the banner and watches his counts go through.

**Success Criteria:**
- He sees each of his queued actions (receipt lines and the delivery completion) with what, how many, when, and a "Not synced" chip.
- When the connection returns, items clear one by one in recorded order without him doing anything (FR-43), ending in "All synced".
- A refused item is never silent: it shows "Not applied – sent to supervisor" with the reason, and he knows he has nothing else to do (FR-44).
- Back returns him to My tasks with the next task (scenario exit).

**Entry Points:**
- Offline banner on any floor screen ([01.1](../01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md), [01.2](../01.2-receive-po/01.2-receive-po.md), …), the scenario path.
- More → Offline queue.
- A "Not applied – sent to supervisor" chip on a task line (opens this page with that item's reason sheet open).

**Exit Points:**
- Back → the screen it came from (here: [01.1 My tasks](../01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md), with the next task) — scenario end.
- Item row → (online, synced items are gone) none; refused item → reason sheet.
- My record (S20) link in the refused-item sheet, where refused items stay listed with their outcome (FR-44, FR-51).

## Technical Notes

- **Source of truth:** the on-device queue (e.g. IndexedDB) with each action's unique ID (FR-14), original device time, User, Warehouse and payload; server time is added on sync (FR-43).
- **Order:** send strictly in recorded order; a refused item does not block later items (each is applied or refused on its own) [confirm with architecture].
- **Never lose an item:** refused items stay on the device list until the server confirms the Sync conflict Issue exists; they remain visible in My record afterwards.
- **Line vs finish actions (DD-1 amended):** a queued receipt, pick or Transfer receipt line is a Stock movement in its own right and is applied or refused on its own; its finish action carries no line quantities and only closes the task and raises shortfall / difference Issues on the server.
- **Draft transfer (DD-5):** a queued draft line carries no Stock movement and is never refused for Available; the queued **Transfer sent** is one action that, when applied, posts one movement per draft line and is refused as a whole if any line no longer fits [ASSUMPTION: all-or-nothing, see 05.2]. Draft lines always sync before their Send (recorded order).
- **Count lines (DD-6):** a queued count line carries no Stock movement; the server records it against the open count. If the count is already closed it never changes the closed count and the server raises a Late count line Issue for the Manager who started it (counted vs On hand, with times).
- **Author identity (DD-8):** every queued action is stamped at recording time with its author and a signed per-session action token, and syncs as that author even after sign-out; the queue shows another author's actions only as a count `[ASSUMPTION: signed action tokens to be confirmed in architecture]`.
- **Blind safety:** the queue payload for receipts, Transfer receipts and count lines holds only the User's own counts.
- **Live regions:** sync progress to polite (batched); refusals to assertive once per item.
- **Background sync:** sync runs whenever the app is open and online, regardless of the current screen; this page only displays it.
- **No manual "Sync now" or delete controls** (FR-43 automatic; nothing may be dropped silently).

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let a distributor owner create his Business and his own Owner account in one short form (Business name, his name, phone number, password), then show his one-time **recovery code** so he can get back in if he forgets his password (FR-1, FR-54). He then lands on the Owner view in its first-use state.

**User Situation:** Mr. Haque, Friday evening at home on his laptop, with his product list and supplier list open in Excel. He opened the Mojud link he was sent and tapped Sign up. Hope: have his real stock in Mojud by Saturday morning. Worry: wasting an evening on a tool his staff won't use, or entering everything twice. He is a skeptical, careful businessman who will judge Mojud by how professional the first minute feels ([persona](../../../B-Trigger-Map/personas/03-Haque-the-Owner.md)).

**Success Criteria:**
- He creates the Business in under 2 minutes, with four fields and one button, and his browser's password manager offers to save the password.
- He sees the recovery code once, in groups, understands it will not be shown again, copies it with one click, and continues with "I have saved it".
- No field error blames him; every error says what to do ("Enter a mobile number, like 01712 345678").
- Business success: the first step of onboarding (SM-7) completes without a consultant.

**Entry Points:**
- The Mojud link he was sent → Sign in page ([10.1 Sign in](../../10-karim-signs-in-on-the-shared-phone/10.1-sign-in/10.1-sign-in.md)) → "Create a business" link; or a direct `/signup` link.

**Exit Points:**
- "I have saved it" → [04.1 Owner view](../../04-haque-traces-an-unexplained-loss/04.1-owner-view/04.1-owner-view.md), first-use state with the setup checklist (`owner-view-first-use`). Not re-specified here.
- "Sign in instead" → [10.1 Sign in](../../10-karim-signs-in-on-the-shared-phone/10.1-sign-in/10.1-sign-in.md).

Desk surface, not signed in: **no app bar navigation, no side nav, no tab bar, no Scan bar** (DD-4: Scan bar is on floor screens only). A slim top strip holds the brand and the language switch; one centered card holds the form. Breakpoints from EXPERIENCE.md → Responsive & Platform (phone 320–599px, tablet 600–1023px, desktop ≥ 1024px [ASSUMPTION in source]).

- **Phone:** no card frame; content fills the width inside `{spacing.gutter-phone}` on `surface`. Buttons full width.

### Form States

- **Valid:** no error indicators; Create business enabled.
- **Invalid:** error fields have a 2px `danger` border and message; Create business stays enabled (it re-runs validation and moves focus to the error summary, or to the field when only one is wrong) so keyboard users are never stuck on a dead button.
- **Submitting:** spinner in the button, "Creating…", fields read-only, no Cancel (request < 2 s); a second click does nothing (FR-14).

## Technical Notes

- **Security (NFR-6):** HTTPS only; password stored as a secure hash; the recovery code stored only as a hash and shown exactly once in View B. The code is never in the URL, page title, logs or analytics. View B sets `Cache-Control: no-store` so Back / reload never shows it again [ASSUMPTION].
- **Account creation:** one transaction creates the Business, the Owner User (Role Owner, all Warehouses, language from the switch) and the recovery code (FR-1, FR-54). Every later record carries this Business's ID (NFR-7).
- **Phone storage:** stored as +8801XXXXXXXXX; displayed as typed groups "01712 345678". Phone is the sign-in identifier, so it must be unique across all Businesses (Open Question 2).
- **Password managers:** `<form>` with real `name`/`id`, `autocomplete="organization"`, `name`, `tel`, `new-password`; the submit is a real submit button so managers offer to save. No paste blocking anywhere. Kept on purpose under DD-8: Owners on personal devices keep password-manager support; only a device marked Shared phone turns it off, on sign-in (10.1).
- **Focus and announcements:** focus on H1 at load and when View B appears; one polite region for "Copied" and "{Business} is ready"; the error summary takes focus on a failed submit, so its count sentence and first error are read once per submit (no `role="alert"`, to avoid a double announcement).
- **Language:** `lang="bn"` / `lang="en"` on the page; the switch segments carry their own `lang`. User-entered names are not marked.
- **Reflow:** works at 320 CSS px and 400% zoom; the recovery-code groups wrap two per line; no fixed heights.
- **No Scan bar, no tab bar, no side nav** on this page (DD-4; not signed in).
- **After "I have saved it":** `location.replace` to the Owner view so the code view leaves history. The one-time "Install app" prompt (EXPERIENCE.md → PWA) is not shown here; it waits for the first normal sign-in [ASSUMPTION].

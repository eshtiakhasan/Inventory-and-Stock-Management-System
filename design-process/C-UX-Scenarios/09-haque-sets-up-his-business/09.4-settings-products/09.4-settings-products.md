## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let the Owner or Manager check and maintain the Product catalogue: create and edit Products (name, SKU, category, Base unit, optional Pack unit with Conversion factor, Barcodes, Low-stock threshold per Warehouse), enter opening stock with a Unit cost (FR-16), archive Products no longer sold (FR-7), and link an unknown Barcode found by a scan to the right Product (FR-38).

**User Situation:** Mr. Haque has just imported 48 Products from Excel (09.3). He wants to make sure they are right before his staff start scanning on Saturday: cartons vs pieces, barcodes, low-stock thresholds and opening stock. He skims the list, fixes a few, then moves on to suppliers. Later, his Manager uses this page when a worker's scan says "Not found".

**Success Criteria:**
- He can spot imported Products that still need attention (no barcode, no threshold, no opening stock) with one filter, without opening each one.
- The carton ↔ piece rule is impossible to misread: "1 carton = 24 pieces" is spelled out as he types.
- Opening stock can be entered per Warehouse with one Unit cost, and the form says plainly that it becomes a permanent record.
- Archiving a Product with stock is refused with the reason and the quantities.
- Linking a scanned unknown Barcode takes: choose Product → confirm carton or piece → Save.

**Entry Points:**
- [09.3 Excel import](../09.3-settings-excel-import/09.3-settings-excel-import.md) result → "Check products" (scenario path).
- [04.1 Owner view](../../04-haque-traces-an-unexplained-loss/04.1-owner-view/04.1-owner-view.md) first-use step 2 secondary link "or add one by one".
- Settings section link "Products"; phone: More → Settings → Products.
- [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md) "Link this barcode to a product" (`scan-message-link-barcode`), Manager / Owner only → barcode link mode.

**Exit Points:**
- Setup strip "Next: Add suppliers" → [09.5 Settings: Suppliers](../09.5-settings-suppliers/09.5-settings-suppliers.md) (scenario exit: "Moves on to suppliers").
- "Import from Excel" → [09.3](../09.3-settings-excel-import/09.3-settings-excel-import.md).
- "View stock" on a Product → [07.2 Product stock](../../07-tanvir-promises-stock-while-the-customer-waits/07.2-product-stock/07.2-product-stock.md) (stock ladder, movement history, Reversal entry).
- Barcode link mode → back to where the scan happened.

## Technical Notes

- **Permissions (FR-4):** Owner and Manager; server-enforced. Other Roles never see this page; the barcode link entry in 01.3 is hidden for them.
- **Units (FR-6, FR-9):** quantities stored in Base units; displayed "120 ctn + 6 pcs". Unit-change lock once movements exist [ASSUMPTION] protects history readability.
- **Opening stock (FR-16, FR-18, FR-11):** Move, Reason "opening balance", into Available; sets Unit cost per Base unit (before the first Receipt). Saved in the same transaction as the Product edit; never edited afterwards (FR-11).
- **Thresholds (DD-3):** low = Promisable ≤ threshold, per Warehouse; empty = none. Feeds the Owner view tile (04.1) and Low stock (04.2).
- **Alternate names (DD-10):** stored per Product (0–5), Unicode-normalised (NFC), indexed for search with the main name, SKU and Barcode on the server and in each phone's catalogue (refreshed on next sync). The same alternate name may appear on several Products (e.g. "চাল" on every rice). No transliteration. Imported through the optional "Alternate names" column (09.3).
- **Barcode link (FR-38):** the link saves only online; the catalogue on each phone refreshes on its next sync (EXPERIENCE.md → Foundation → Offline data).
- **Scan buttons:** open the Scan overlay (01.3) in fill-the-field mode; a scan fills, never saves. USB / Bluetooth scanners act as keyboards. In a barcode field, Enter (which most scanners send after the code) moves focus to the next field instead of submitting the form, so a scan never saves by itself [ASSUMPTION].
- **No Scan bar** (DD-4).
- **Performance:** list virtualisation is not used for screen-reader reasons; paginate at 100 rows with "Show more" (no infinite scroll) [ASSUMPTION].
- **Focus:** row → form title; Save → the edited row; link mode → search field, then the unit chips.

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let the Owner or Manager add the people who will use Mojud, each with a name, phone number, **Role**, **Assigned Warehouses** and a **temporary password** (FR-3); change a person's Role or Warehouses; reset a forgotten password to a new temporary one (FR-54); and deactivate someone who has left. Managers cannot create, change or deactivate Owners, and the Business always keeps one active Owner (FR-3).

**User Situation:** Mr. Haque, still on his laptop on Friday evening, adds Nasima (Supervisor), Rahim and Karim (Warehouse workers) and Tanvir (Sales rep). He will hand each person their temporary password in person on Saturday morning; Mojud sends no SMS (FR-2). His fear: a tool staff won't use. He wants each person to see only what they need.

**Success Criteria:**
- Adding a person takes: name, phone, one Role chip, Warehouse chips, Save. The temporary password is made for him.
- Each Role says in one line what that person will be able to do, so he picks the right one without knowing FR-4.
- After Save he sees, once, exactly what to hand over: name, phone, temporary password, and that they will set their own at first sign-in.
- He can reset a password in two clicks when someone forgets theirs.
- He cannot lock the Business out of itself (no deactivating the last Owner, no deactivating himself).

**Entry Points:**
- [04.1 Owner view](../../04-haque-traces-an-unexplained-loss/04.1-owner-view/04.1-owner-view.md) first-use step 5 "Add users".
- Setup strip "Next: Add users" (09.5 or 09.3 after opening stock).
- Settings section link "Users"; phone: More → Settings → Users (scenario path from 09.5).

**Exit Points:**
- Side nav "Orders" → Purchase orders segment → [09.7 Purchase orders](../09.7-purchase-orders/09.7-purchase-orders.md) (scenario exit: "Opens Purchase orders"). On phone the Owner uses the Orders tab (Overview · Issues · Stock · Orders · More); the Manager uses More → Orders (DD-9).
- New User's first sign-in → [10.1 Sign in](../../10-karim-signs-in-on-the-shared-phone/10.1-sign-in/10.1-sign-in.md) → [10.2 Change temporary password](../../10-karim-signs-in-on-the-shared-phone/10.2-change-temporary-password/10.2-change-temporary-password.md) (on their own device; not a navigation from this page).

## Technical Notes

- **Permissions (FR-3, FR-4):** server-enforced. Managers: no Owner option, no edits / reset / deactivate on Owners. Nobody resets their own password here.
- **Passwords (NFR-6, FR-54):** temporary passwords are hashed on save; the plain text exists only in the Pass it on panel of this browser session and is never stored, logged or sent again. The User must change it at next sign-in (10.2); "Must set password" status comes from that flag.
- **Deactivation (FR-3):** a deactivated User cannot sign in; open sessions end at their next request [ASSUMPTION]. Past Stock movements keep their name (FR-3, FR-11).
- **Role changes** affect FR-5 checks only for future actions; past records are unchanged.
- **Shared phones** (EXPERIENCE.md → Responsive & Platform; PRD §9 Q2; DD-8): nothing on this page assigns devices; each User signs in with their own phone number. The device's Shared phone setting is asked at the first sign-in on that device (10.1), on by default for floor Roles, and changed in Settings: Me (10.3), not here. The Pass it on panel tells the admin to ask staff not to save the password on a shared phone.
- **Reset and queued actions (DD-8):** actions already queued on a phone keep their author stamp and signed action token, so a reset or a new sign-in on that phone never changes who recorded them.
- **No Scan bar** (DD-4); settings need internet.
- **Focus:** Save on Add → Pass it on headline; Done → the new row; dialogs → Go back.
- **Reflow:** Role options stack full width; Warehouse chips wrap; the password display wraps at 320 CSS px.

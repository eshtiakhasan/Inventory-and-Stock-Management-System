## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let the Owner or Manager create a Purchase order for one Supplier and one Warehouse (Products, ordered quantities, expected date, unit purchase cost per Pack or Base unit, FR-20), send it so it becomes a receipt task on the phones at that Warehouse (FR-21, FR-53), follow it through Partly received and Received as lines are counted (FR-22, DD-1), and close a Partly received PO short, which raises a Shortfall Issue (FR-22).

**User Situation:** Mr. Haque has finished Settings. It is late Friday evening; tomorrow morning a delivery from his usual rice supplier is due at Narayanganj. He creates his first PO, sends it, and wants proof that Karim will see it as a task in the morning. That is the moment Mojud stops being a setup exercise and starts running his business.

**Success Criteria:**
- Creating a PO with 3 lines takes under 2 minutes: Supplier, Warehouse, lines with quantity and cost, expected date, Send.
- Cost can be typed per carton (as the supplier quotes it) and is shown per piece too, with the order total in taka.
- Before sending, he sees who at that Warehouse can receive it, and that he cannot receive it himself (FR-5).
- After Send, the screen says plainly that it is now a receipt task at Narayanganj (scenario climax: "it appears as a receipt task on the Narayanganj phones").
- Close short is impossible to do by accident: one confirmation dialog states the lines and quantities that will be closed.

**Entry Points:**
- Desktop side nav **Orders** → Purchase orders segment (scenario path from 09.6).
- Phone / tablet: Owner taps the **Orders** tab (tabs Overview · Issues · Stock · Orders · More, DD-9), then the Purchase orders segment. Manager (no Orders tab): More → **Orders**, the first item in More (DD-9).
- [04.2 Low stock](../../04-haque-traces-an-unexplained-loss/04.2-low-stock/04.2-low-stock.md) → "Create purchase order" for a low Product (pre-fills that line) [ASSUMPTION].
- Shortfall / Over-delivery Issue detail ([03.2](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md)) → link to its PO.
- [01.2 Receive PO](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.2-receive-po/01.2-receive-po.md) header (Manager / Owner) → "View purchase order".

**Exit Points:**
- After Send, the scenario ends: [09: Mr. Haque Sets Up His Business](../09-haque-sets-up-his-business.md). The PO appears in My tasks at Narayanganj ([01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md)) and is received blind on [01.2 Receive PO](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.2-receive-po/01.2-receive-po.md).
- Linked Issue rows → [03.2 Issue detail](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md).
- Other Orders segments → [07.3 Dispatch orders](../../07-tanvir-promises-stock-while-the-customer-waits/07.3-dispatch-orders/07.3-dispatch-orders.md), [05.1 Transfers](../../05-karims-transfer-arrives-short/05.1-transfers-list/05.1-transfers-list.md).
- "Add a new supplier" → [09.5](../09.5-settings-suppliers/09.5-settings-suppliers.md) (returns to the draft).

## Form Validation

Save draft checks only Supplier and Warehouse; Send checks everything. Line errors in the summary name the line: "Line 2: Unit cost missing".

### Validation Rules

| Field | Rule | Error Code | Error Message (EN) |
|-------|------|------------|--------------------|
| Supplier | Required (draft and send); active | `ERR_PO_SUPPLIER_REQUIRED` | "Choose a supplier." |
| Warehouse | Required (draft and send); active | `ERR_PO_WAREHOUSE_REQUIRED` | "Choose the warehouse that will receive it." |

| Lines | At least one complete line to send | `ERR_PO_NO_LINES` | "Add at least one product." |

| Line Quantity | Whole numbers; total > 0 | `ERR_PO_LINE_QTY` | "Line {n}: Enter a quantity." |
| Line Unit cost | Required; > 0; up to 2 decimals | `ERR_PO_LINE_COST` | "Line {n}: Enter the unit cost." |
| Cancel (server) | No receipt recorded yet | `ERR_PO_CANCEL_RECEIVED` | "Something has already been received on {poNo}. Use Close short instead." |

## Technical Notes

- **Permissions (FR-4):** Owner and Manager; server-enforced. FR-5: the creator can never record Receipts on this PO (the receive screen refuses with "You created this purchase order, so someone else must receive it." [01.2]). Owner self-approval exception (FR-5) does not apply to receiving [ASSUMPTION].
- **Statuses (FR-20, FR-22):** Draft → Sent → Partly received → Received, or Closed short (from Partly received), or Cancelled (from Draft or Sent with no receipt). Status updates automatically as lines are saved on S7 (DD-1: each Save line is its own Stock movement; Mark delivery complete raises any Shortfall).
- **Costs (FR-18, FR-20):** stored per Base unit; the Receipt uses the PO line cost (FR-21) and may record a Revalue (FR-18). Taka only to Stock-value Roles (DD-2): this page is Owner / Manager only.
- **Tasks (FR-53):** Send makes a receipt task visible in My tasks for floor Users at that Warehouse, including on phones after their next sync; offline phones see it after reconnecting ("A task assigned while the phone was offline appears after the next sync", EXPERIENCE.md → State Patterns).
- **Blind (FR-21):** ordered quantities appear only on this desk page; they never reach S7's page code.
- **URL state:** `/orders/purchase`, `/orders/purchase/new`, `/orders/purchase/{poNo}`; Back from detail keeps list scroll and filters.
- **No Scan bar** (DD-4); Product fields have scan buttons.
- **Focus:** Create → H1 then Supplier; Add line → new Product field; Remove → next line's Product or Add line; Send → detail heading; dialogs → Go back.
- **Reflow:** below 600px the line table becomes line cards; amounts never truncate; long Supplier names wrap.

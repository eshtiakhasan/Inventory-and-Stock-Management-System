## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Find any Product in a few keystrokes, in Bangla or English, by main name, alternate name, SKU or Barcode (DD-10), and see **how much is Promisable at each Warehouse right in the result row**, so a Sales rep can answer a customer without opening anything else (FR-8, UJ-4 steps 1-2).

**User Situation:** Tanvir, Sales rep, is in a rickshaw between shops when a regular retailer calls: "50 cartons of Rice 25 kg, delivered tomorrow?" The customer is waiting on the line. He holds the phone in one hand, the call on speaker. He hopes to say yes on the call and have it hold. He worries about promising stock that is damaged, or already promised to a colleague's customer ([scenario 07](../07-tanvir-promises-stock-while-the-customer-waits.md), Q3-Q4; [Trigger Map → Supporting groups](../../../B-Trigger-Map/trigger-map.md)).

**Success Criteria:**
- Tanvir sees "Narayanganj 32 ctn · Gazipur 20 ctn Promisable" within 10 seconds of opening Mojud (SM-6), with results appearing while he types (< 1 s, NFR-1).
- The figures are **Promisable** (On hand − Damaged − Reserved), never On hand, so damaged or already reserved stock is never offered twice (FR-12).
- Every figure carries its freshness ("as of 10:42"), and offline it says so ("as of 10:42 (offline)") (FR-12, FR-42).
- Low and out-of-stock Warehouses are marked with an icon and text, using Promisable at or below the threshold (DD-3).
- No taka amount appears on this page for any Role.
- A User assigned to an open count sees "Hidden during count" instead of figures and low-stock markers for the Products in that count, at that Warehouse (DD-7).

**Entry Points:**
- Sales rep: opens Mojud; home is Product search (Stock tab) (EXPERIENCE.md → Home and navigation per Role).
- Manager, Owner, Accountant, Supervisor: Stock tab / side-nav "Stock".
- Warehouse worker: not on his tabs; reached through Type code in the Scan overlay, which searches the same way ([01.3](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md)). See Open Question 3.
- Back from [07.2 Product stock](../07.2-product-stock/07.2-product-stock.md): the query and the result list are kept, scroll kept, focus on the result that was opened.

**Exit Points:**
- Tap a result row → [07.2 Product stock](../07.2-product-stock/07.2-product-stock.md) for that Product (scenario's next step).
- Scan button in the field → [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md); a matched Barcode opens 07.2 directly (Interaction Primitives: "S5 (no mode) → opens Product stock").
- Tab bar / side nav → Orders ([07.3 Dispatch orders](../07.3-dispatch-orders/07.3-dispatch-orders.md)), Issues, More.

## Layout Structure

Desk-style home screen (DD-4): sticky app bar, the search field directly under it, an "as of" line, then the live result list. **No fixed Scan bar** for any Role on this page (S5 is not one of the floor screens S3, S6–S11); scanning is the 48px scan button inside the field. Bottom tab bar on phone; side nav on desktop.

## Technical Notes

- **Search sources:** online, the server answers (< 1 s, NFR-1); the phone's cached catalogue answers first so results appear even before the server replies. Offline, only the cached catalogue and last-known stock are used (EXPERIENCE.md → Foundation → Offline data).
- **Matching:** case-insensitive; Bangla and English (FR-8); Unicode-normalised (NFC) so Bangla typed with different keyboards matches; SKU prefix and exact Barcode. Each Product's main name and its Alternate names (up to 5, either language, DD-10) are all searchable, on the server and in the phone's catalogue; the result row reports which Alternate name matched. No automatic transliteration.
- **Blind count (DD-7):** for a viewer assigned to an open count, the search response omits figures and low status for the counted Products at that Warehouse and flags them as hidden; the phone's cached stock for those Product-Warehouse pairs is not shown either until Close count.
- **Figures:** Promisable per Warehouse = On hand − Damaged − Reserved, computed from the Stock ledger (FR-11, FR-12); shown in Pack + Base units (FR-9). The search response carries **no Stock value** for any Role.
- **Low-stock rule (DD-3):** compare Promisable with the per-Warehouse Low-stock threshold (FR-6); ≤ threshold = low; 0 = out of stock.
- **Accessibility:** combobox pattern (`role="combobox"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`); results are links (not `option`s) so the row behaves as a normal link for TalkBack [ASSUMPTION; confirm pattern in build]. One polite live region announces the result count after a pause. Focus ring per `focus-ring`. Rows 64px; scan and clear 48px with `{spacing.2}` between.
- **Language:** page `lang="bn"` or `lang="en"`; Product and Warehouse names are not marked or translated.
- **Back:** query and scroll are kept in history state (e.g. `/stock?q=rice`) so Back from 07.2 restores them.
- **Performance target:** SM-6 is ≤ 10 s from opening Mojud to reading Promisable; the home must render the field without waiting for the network.

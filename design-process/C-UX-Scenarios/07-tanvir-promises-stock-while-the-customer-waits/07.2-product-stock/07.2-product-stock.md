## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show, for one Product, the **stock ladder at every Warehouse** the User can see (On hand − Damaged − Reserved = **Promisable**, plus In transit and Unexplained), when it was last verified by a count, its Stock value for the Roles allowed to see money, and its full **movement history** with filters; and give the next action for the Role: Create Dispatch order (sales), Flag damage (floor), Request Reversal (Owner, Manager) (FR-12, FR-17, FR-18, FR-15).

**User Situation:** Tanvir tapped "Rice 25 kg" in the results with the customer still on the line. He already knows Narayanganj has 32 Promisable and Gazipur 20; here he confirms nothing is hiding behind those numbers (damaged and reserved are subtracted in plain sight) and starts the order from the Narayanganj ladder. He worries about promising stock that is damaged or already promised; the ladder shows him exactly that. Mr. Haque, at the end of his setup (UJ-8 step 9), sees the same page for the first time with real opening stock and Stock value in taka.

**Success Criteria:**
- The Promisable figure per Warehouse is the largest number on the card (`qty-display`) and every step to it is visible: On hand, − Damaged, − Reserved (FR-12).
- Every figure shows freshness "as of HH:MM", offline "as of HH:MM (offline)" (FR-12, FR-42).
- Tanvir starts a Dispatch order for the right Warehouse in one tap from that Warehouse's card (UJ-4 step 3).
- Owner, Manager and Accountant see Stock value per Warehouse and in total; Sales reps, Supervisors and Warehouse workers never see a taka amount, not even in page code (FR-4, DD-2).
- Owner / Manager can request a Reversal of a movement with a Reason in one sheet; the original and its Reversal both stay visible (FR-15, FR-11).

**Entry Points:**
- Result row on [07.1 Product search](../07.1-product-search/07.1-product-search.md) (this scenario).
- A scan with no mode: Scan tab, My tasks Scan bar, or this page's own Scan bar ([01.3](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md)).
- Owner view drill-down Stock-movement row ([04.1](../../04-haque-traces-an-unexplained-loss/04.1-owner-view/04.1-owner-view.md)): opens with history filtered to that movement and the row highlighted.
- Low stock row ([04.2](../../04-haque-traces-an-unexplained-loss/04.2-low-stock/04.2-low-stock.md)): that Warehouse's ladder first.
- Issue detail evidence link to a movement ([03.2](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md)).
- UJ-8 step 9: Mr. Haque searches Rice 25 kg after setup (scenario [09](../../09-haque-sets-up-his-business/09-haque-sets-up-his-business.md)).

**Exit Points:**
- Create Dispatch order (per Warehouse) → [07.3 Dispatch orders](../07.3-dispatch-orders/07.3-dispatch-orders.md), create form with this Product and Warehouse pre-filled (scenario's next step).
- Flag damage (floor Roles) → Scan overlay in Flag damage mode, flag from Available at the User's Warehouse ([02.2 Flag damage](../../02-karim-picks-and-flags-damage/02.2-flag-damage/02.2-flag-damage.md)).
- Movement source link → the PO, Dispatch order, Transfer, Stock count, Damage flag or Issue (read-only where the Role cannot act).
- Request Reversal → sheet → a Reversal request Issue decided by a different Owner or Manager in [03.2](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md).
- Back → the list it came from (07.1 with query kept, 04.1 panel, 04.2, My tasks).

## Layout Structure

App bar with Back and the Product name; a Product header (SKU, units, as-of, and for value Roles the total Stock value); one **ladder card per Warehouse**; then the **movement history** with a filter control. The bottom depends on the Role:

- **Floor Roles (worker, Supervisor):** fixed bottom zone = Flag damage button (64px) + Scan bar (72px), `{spacing.3}` padding, 1px `border` top (DD-4, DESIGN.md → Layout).
- **Desk Roles on phone:** bottom tab bar only (no Scan bar, DD-4).
- **Desktop:** side nav; ladder cards in a row (up to 3 Warehouses, FR-10), history below as a wider list.

## Technical Notes

- **Figures (FR-12):** every ladder figure is the sum of the Product's Stock movements per state and Warehouse; Promisable = On hand − Damaged − Reserved. Shown in Pack + Base units (FR-9).
- **Value is a server-side rule (FR-4, DD-2):** Stock value, Unit cost, Revalue movements and per-row values are omitted from the API response for Sales rep, Supervisor and Warehouse worker, not just hidden in the UI. Value uses the Unit cost stored on each movement for history rows (FR-18) and the current Unit cost for the ladders.
- **Low stock (DD-3):** Promisable ≤ per-Warehouse threshold.
- **Last verified (FR-36):** latest Stock count line at this Warehouse recorded as "verified".
- **Reversal (FR-15):** the request creates a Reversal request Issue; the opposite movement is recorded only on approval by a different Owner or Manager (FR-5), linked to the original; both stay visible. FR-13 is checked at request (advisory) and at approval (binding). Device-generated action ID (FR-14).
- **Accessibility:** the ladder is a list of rows with text values; the bar is `aria-hidden`. Ladder rows are buttons that set a filter and move focus to the History heading (announce "History filtered: Narayanganj, Damaged"). Request Reversal and the source link are siblings, never nested. Sheet traps focus and returns it. Floor Roles: focus returns to the Scan bar after a Flag damage save.
- **URL state:** `/stock/{productId}?warehouse=…&state=…&reason=…&movement=…` so Back and the 04.1 drill-down restore the view.
- **Offline:** ladders from the phone's last-known stock (Assigned Warehouses), history from cache; Flag damage queues (FR-41).
- **Blind count (DD-7):** for a viewer assigned to an open count, the API omits On hand, Promisable, the ladder, low status and movement quantities for the counted Products at that Warehouse and returns a "hidden during count" flag instead; the phone's cached figures for those pairs are not shown either. Restriction ends at Close count.

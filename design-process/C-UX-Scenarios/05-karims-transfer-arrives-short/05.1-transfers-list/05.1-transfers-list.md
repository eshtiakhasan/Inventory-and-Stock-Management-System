## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show the Transfers that touch the User's Warehouses, split into **Incoming**, **Outgoing** and **Drafts** (saved, not sent yet, DD-5), each with a plain status (In transit, Received, Short, Overdue, Not synced, Draft · not sent), and start a new Transfer with **Send Transfer** (FR-26–FR-28).

**User Situation:** Karim, Monday 10 am at Narayanganj. The company pickup is leaving for Gazipur with 20 cartons of Rice 25 kg. He opens More → Transfers to send them. He hopes the record will show that 20 left his hands; he worries that if Gazipur ends up short, everyone assumes he sent less.

**Success Criteria:**
- Send Transfer is one tap away and opens [05.2 Send Transfer](../05.2-send-transfer/05.2-send-transfer.md) with the destination already chosen when only one other Warehouse exists.
- Every Transfer row says where it goes or comes from, who sent it and when, and its status in words with an icon (never color alone).
- **Blind stays blind:** an incoming Transfer that is still In transit never shows the sent quantity to anyone who may receive it (FR-27). After receipt, the receiver does not see "Short" on his own receipt (State Patterns → Blind).
- An outgoing Transfer with a shortage shows "Short" to the sender, so Karim can see his part is on record (the scenario's Worry).
- A draft is never lost from view: every unsent draft is listed under **Drafts** with who saved it and when, and can be reopened or discarded (DD-5).

**Entry Points:**
- More → Transfers (Warehouse worker, Supervisor; EXPERIENCE.md → Home and navigation per Role).
- Orders → Transfers segment: Orders tab for the Owner; More → Orders (first More item) for the Manager (DD-9; [07.3 Dispatch orders](../../07-tanvir-promises-stock-while-the-customer-waits/07.3-dispatch-orders/07.3-dispatch-orders.md) defines Orders).
- Back from [05.2 Send Transfer](../05.2-send-transfer/05.2-send-transfer.md) (Manager / Owner only; floor roles land on My tasks after sending, FR-53).
- Back from [05.3 Receive Transfer](../05.3-receive-transfer/05.3-receive-transfer.md) or [03.2 Issue detail](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md), scroll kept.

**Exit Points:**
- **Send Transfer** → [05.2 Send Transfer](../05.2-send-transfer/05.2-send-transfer.md) (scenario's next step).
- Incoming row, In transit → [05.3 Receive Transfer](../05.3-receive-transfer/05.3-receive-transfer.md).
- Drafts row → [05.2 Send Transfer](../05.2-send-transfer/05.2-send-transfer.md) with the draft reopened (DD-5).
- Row with an Issue (Short, Overdue, Extra) → [03.2 Issue detail](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md) (decide or view only).
- Other rows → Transfer detail, read-only (not yet specified; Open Question 3).
- Offline banner → [01.4 Offline queue](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).
- Tabs → other home surfaces.

## Layout Structure

A list screen, not a task screen. Sticky app bar, optional offline banner, a full-width **Send Transfer** button, an **Incoming / Outgoing / Drafts** segment control (DD-5), then the Transfer rows. Per DD-4 there is **no fixed Scan bar** here (S16 is not one of S3, S6–S11); the fixed bottom zone holds only the bottom tab bar, with **More** active for floor roles and the Manager, and **Orders** active for the Owner (DD-9). Scanning is reached through the Scan tab.

## Technical Notes

- **Blind is a data rule:** the list payload for a User who may receive a Transfer (floor roles at the destination; desk roles too, since they may receive) carries **no sent quantities** for In transit Transfers, and the receiver's own rows never carry a Short / Extra status (State Patterns → Blind; FR-27). Quantities are fetched only by Transfer detail for the sender and by Issue detail for deciders.
- **Statuses are server-derived** from the Transfer and its Issues (FR-28); the phone derives only `notSynced` for Transfers sent from this phone and `draft` for drafts kept only on this phone (DD-5).
- **Overdue** is measured from the time **Send Transfer** was finished (server time when synced; device time while queued) [ASSUMPTION; FR-28 provisional 48 h].
- **Scope:** floor roles see Transfers where the source or destination is an Assigned Warehouse; Manager / Owner see all (FR-4, Glossary → Assigned Warehouses).
- **No ৳** on this page for any Role (DD-2 applies to floor roles; Transfers carry no value column for desk roles either in v1) [ASSUMPTION].
- **Routing:** each row is one `<a>`; status tag is text inside it; the refused-sync chip is a sibling button. Focus moves to the `h1` on arrival; Back restores scroll and focus to the row that was opened.
- **Live regions:** sync and online/offline changes go to the polite region.

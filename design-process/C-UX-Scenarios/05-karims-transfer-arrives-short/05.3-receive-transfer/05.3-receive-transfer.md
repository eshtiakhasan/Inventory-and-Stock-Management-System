## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let the receiver count what arrived, line by line, **without ever seeing the sent quantity**, save each count under his name, and close the receipt with one confirm. Mojud compares the counts with what was sent and raises any Transfer shortage or overage Issue to the destination Supervisor, not to the receiver (FR-27, FR-28).

**User Situation:** Jamal, a Warehouse worker at Gazipur, Monday 16:40. The company pickup has backed in with the cartons Karim loaded at 10:12. Jamal opens the "Incoming Transfer TR-0087" task and counts 19 cartons of Rice 25 kg. He does not know that 20 were sent, and he should not need to.

**Success Criteria:**
- He receives a line with scan + count + one tap on Save line (blind exception to NFR-2).
- No sent, expected or system quantity appears anywhere: on screen, after save, in the dialog, in `aria-*`, hidden text or page code (State Patterns → Blind; FR-27).
- **Finish receipt** is available at any time and confirms in one dialog that counts lines, never quantities.
- After confirming he lands on My tasks with "Receipt saved · TR-0087" and the next task. The Transfer-shortage Issue (sent 20 by Karim at 10:12 / received 19 by Jamal at 16:40, 1 ctn still In transit) appears in the Gazipur Supervisor's inbox ([03.1](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.1-issues-inbox/03.1-issues-inbox.md), [03.2](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md)), never on Jamal's screen.
- Karim, if he opens this Transfer (e.g. assigned to both Warehouses), is refused in one plain sentence: "You sent this Transfer, so someone else must receive it." (FR-5).

**Entry Points:**
- Incoming Transfer task card on [01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md) (`mytasks-card-transfer`; scenario path at Gazipur).
- Incoming row, In transit, on [05.1 Transfers](../05.1-transfers-list/05.1-transfers-list.md).
- Back from [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md).

**Exit Points:**
- **Finish receipt** → confirm → [01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md) with "Receipt saved · TR-0087" and the next task (scenario ends; Karim's proof continues in [06.1 My record](../../06-karim-proves-where-a-carton-went/06.1-my-record/06.1-my-record.md)).
- Scan bar → [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md) in `blind-receive` context.
- Back (app bar) → origin; saved lines stay saved, task "In progress".
- Offline banner → [01.4 Offline queue](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).

## Technical Notes

- **Blind is a data rule:** the S10 receive payload, including the offline cache, contains Product IDs and names only, **no sent quantity, no sender count, no difference**, in no field, `aria-*`, hidden text or analytics event (FR-27; State Patterns → Blind). This also covers the task card on 01.1 and the incoming row on 05.1.
- **DD-1 movement model:** each Save line moves the counted quantity from In transit to Available at the destination, up to what is still In transit on that line; any extra above the sent quantity is held, not added (FR-28 overage), and is decided later. Finish receipt posts nothing and triggers the FR-28 comparison.
- **Segregation of duties:** checked on open (client hides the lines) and on every save (server refuses; FR-5). If the sender's queued receive line reaches the server it is refused as a Sync conflict.
- **Action IDs:** every Save line and the completion carry a unique device ID (FR-14).
- **Times:** device time and server time stored per line (FR-43); the Issue evidence shows the receiver's finish time (16:40 in the scenario).
- **Live regions, focus, scroll padding:** as 01.2.

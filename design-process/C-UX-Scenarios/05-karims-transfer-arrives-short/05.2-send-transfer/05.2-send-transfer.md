## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let the sender choose where the stock is going, scan and count out each Product, save each line under his name into a **draft**, and finish with one bilingual **Send Transfer** tap. Saved lines move no stock: the quantities stay Available at the source and nothing is reserved. **Send Transfer** posts every line from Available to **In transit** at that moment, one movement per line (FR-26, DD-1 amended, DD-5).

**User Situation:** Karim, Monday 10:12 at Narayanganj, the pickup driver waiting. He has 20 cartons of Rice 25 kg stacked by the door. He wants the 20 on record under his name before the cartons leave his hands.

**Success Criteria:**
- With two Warehouses in the Business, the destination is already chosen; Karim's path is: scan, count 20, **Save line**, **Send Transfer**.
- Every saved line shows "Counted out {qty}" and "Saved" (online) or "Saved on phone, will sync" (offline).
- He can never count out more than is Available; the refusal names the limit in his units ("Only 32 ctn available. Max 32 ctn.", FR-13). The check runs again at Send Transfer: if Available has dropped below a draft line by then, Send is refused for that line with the max (DD-5).
- After Send Transfer he lands on My tasks with "Transfer sent · TR-0087" and the next task (FR-53).
- A draft is never invisible: it shows as "Draft transfer · not sent" in his My tasks and in the 05.1 **Drafts** segment until it is sent or discarded (DD-5).

**Entry Points:**
- **Send Transfer** on [05.1 Transfers](../05.1-transfers-list/05.1-transfers-list.md) (scenario path).
- **Send Transfer** in the header of [01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md).
- A draft Transfer (lines saved, not sent) reopened from the 05.1 **Drafts** segment or the creator's "Draft transfer · not sent" card on 01.1 My tasks (DD-5).
- Back from [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md) (Close, or line added / selected).

**Exit Points:**
- **Send Transfer** (finish) → [01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md) with toast and next task (floor roles); back to [05.1 Transfers](../05.1-transfers-list/05.1-transfers-list.md) for Manager / Owner. Scenario continues at Gazipur in [05.3 Receive Transfer](../05.3-receive-transfer/05.3-receive-transfer.md).
- Scan bar → [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md) in `send-transfer` context.
- Back (app bar) → where he came from; saved lines stay in the draft (no stock moved) and the Transfer stays "Draft · not sent" (DD-5).
- **Discard draft** → back to where he came from; the draft and its lines are removed, no stock ever moved (DD-5).
- Offline banner → [01.4 Offline queue](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).

## Technical Notes

- **Draft movement model (DD-1 amended, DD-5):** each Save line records a draft line (Product, quantity, User, device time, server time on sync, Warehouse) and posts **no** stock; Available is unchanged and nothing is reserved. **Send Transfer** posts one movement per line, Available (source) → In transit, at that moment, linked to the Transfer (FR-11, FR-43, SM-3), makes it receivable at the destination and starts the 48 h overdue clock (FR-28).
- **Draft visibility:** the server raises a **Draft transfer not sent** Issue to the source Supervisor 24 h after the first saved line if the draft is still unsent `[ASSUMPTION: 24 h]` (DD-5; see [03.2](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md)).
- **Action IDs:** each Save line, the Send and Discard draft carry a device-generated unique ID (FR-14); retries apply once. The draft gets a device-generated ID on the first saved line so offline lines can link to it before a server number exists.
- **Offline:** the draft persists on this phone; Save line queues as a draft line, and Send Transfer queues as **one** action (FR-41, DD-5). Queued actions keep their author (DD-8). The max check uses the cached last-known Available for the User's Assigned Warehouse (Foundation → Offline data) and is re-checked on the server at Send.
- **Not blind, but scoped:** this screen shows the sender's own counts and the source's Available; it must never show anything about the destination's stock.
- **Re-selecting a saved line** shows his saved count; because the line is still a draft, changing it edits the draft line before Send (no stock correction needed); the edit is kept in the line's history [ASSUMPTION; Open Question 2].
- **Live regions:** toasts and scan matches polite; refusals assertive, once per change.
- **Focus:** after a scan, the focused unit field; after save, the Scan bar; after a refusal on save, Use max.

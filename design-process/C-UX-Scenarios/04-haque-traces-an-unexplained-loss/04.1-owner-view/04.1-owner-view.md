## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show the Owner, in taka, where stock value sits and where it leaked: Stock value per Warehouse, this month's losses by Reason, the current Unexplained value, open Discrepancy cases and other open Issues, and how many Products are low. Every figure taps through to the Issues or Stock movements behind it (FR-48, FR-49).

**User Situation:** Mr. Haque, Owner, Sunday evening at home on his laptop, doing his weekly look at the business before Monday's supplier calls. Hope: see exactly where taka leaked this month and who will fix it. Worry: finding out too late, or finding a number nobody can explain. He is not in a hurry, but he is skeptical: "Don't tell me stock is short. Tell me which carton, which day, and who counted." ([persona](../../../B-Trigger-Map/personas/03-Haque-the-Owner.md))

**Success Criteria:**
- He reads the losses by Reason and the Unexplained value without scrolling on a 1024 × 768 desktop or doing any arithmetic.
- One click on the Unexplained tile shows the open Discrepancy cases behind ৳1,450, each with Product, Warehouse, quantity, value, the Stock count that found it, who counted and when (FR-49, UJ-6 step 3).
- One click on a case opens Issue detail, where he assigns it to the Manager (FR-33); he returns to the same panel with his place kept.
- The Unexplained value is never shown by color alone (question icon + dashed outline + text).
- Business success: Unexplained case assigned within minutes of being seen (SM-4); the owner sees value in Mojud (SM-7).

**Entry Points:**
- Browser bookmark or installed PWA: Owner view is home for Owner and Accountant (EXPERIENCE.md → Home and navigation per Role).
- Side nav "Overview" (desktop) or bottom tab "Overview" (phone) from any other surface.
- After sign-up, the first-use state (from [09.1 Sign up](../../09-haque-sets-up-his-business/09.1-sign-up/09.1-sign-up.md), UJ-8 step 3).
- After owner recovery ([11: Mr. Haque recovers his account](../../11-haque-recovers-his-account/11-haque-recovers-his-account.md)).
- Back from Issue detail or a Stock movement opened from the drill-down (returns with the same panel open and scroll kept).

**Exit Points:**
- Drill-down row (Issue) → [03.2 Issue detail](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md), where "Assign to…" lives (UJ-6 steps 4-5). Not re-specified here.
- Drill-down row (Stock movement) → Product stock movement history (S6, [07.2 Product stock](../../07-tanvir-promises-stock-while-the-customer-waits/07.2-product-stock/07.2-product-stock.md)) filtered to that movement.
- Low stock tile → [04.2 Low stock](../04.2-low-stock/04.2-low-stock.md).
- First-use checklist steps → Settings pages in scenario 09.
- Side nav (desktop) → Issues, Stock, Orders, Counts, Settings; phone tab bar (Owner) → Issues, Stock, Orders, More (DD-9).

## Layout Structure

Desk surface. Calm Professional density, flat cards on `surface-alt` page background (as mocked). Breakpoints from EXPERIENCE.md → Responsive & Platform: phone 320–599px, tablet 600–1023px, desktop ≥ 1024px [ASSUMPTION in source: breakpoint values].

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** three columns: `side-nav` (13.75rem) | content (two-column tile grid) | `drill-down-panel` (min 22rem, right, 1px `border` left edge). When no drill-down is open, the content area takes the panel's width and the tile grid stays two columns.
- **Tablet (600–1023px):** no side nav (bottom tab bar), two-column tile grid; the drill-down panel opens **below the tiles**, full width, and the page scrolls to its heading.
- **Phone (320–599px):** one column of tiles, bottom tab bar. Tapping a tile opens the drill-down as **its own full-screen view** with a Back button (DESIGN.md → Drill-down panel: "Phone: its own full-screen page"). See Open Question 1: the scenario brief asked for "drill-down below tiles" on phone.

Tile order (reading order = focus order). The order puts the two figures of UJ-6 (losses, Unexplained) first on a phone:

1. Losses by Reason (with month selector)
2. Unexplained, now
3. Open discrepancy cases and issues
4. Stock value
5. Low stock

On desktop the two-column grid keeps the mockup's visual placement where it does not fight reading order: left column = Stock value, Unexplained, Open cases; right column = Losses by Reason, Low stock. DOM order follows the list above; CSS grid places them (see Open Question 4).

## Technical Notes

- **Permissions (FR-4):** server returns Stock value, losses and Owner view only to Owner, Manager, Accountant. Buttons and items for other Roles are hidden; deep links show "You don't have access to this."
- **Figures:** losses by Reason are Write-off movements in the chosen calendar month, valued at the Unit cost stored on each movement (FR-18), so past months never change. Unexplained, open Issues and Stock value are "now". **Unexplained changes only at Close count** (DD-6): saved count lines move no stock while the count is open, so the tile and its drill-down never show partial results of a running count; count lines that sync after close become Late count line Issues (counted in open Issues, not in Unexplained). Taka with ৳ and lakh grouping in both languages (৳12,45,000); Western digits; tabular numerals.
- **Drill-down data (FR-49):** each row carries Product, quantity (in units, FR-9), value, User and time. Sort: highest value first [ASSUMPTION in EXPERIENCE.md]. Lists are finite; paginate with a "Show more" button if over 50 rows (no infinite scroll, Interaction Primitives → Banned) [new assumption, Open Question 12].
- **URL state:** the open panel and month are in the URL (e.g. `/overview?month=2026-10&drill=unexplained`) so Back from Issue detail restores the panel, and the phone full-screen view is a real history entry (Android Back closes it).
- **Focus:** panel open → focus to panel heading; Close/Back → focus to the source tile/row; route change → focus to H1. Tile buttons use `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. One polite live region announces month changes and "Assigned to …" on return. Focus ring per `focus-ring` (two-tone, never `primary`).
- **Landmarks:** skip link, `nav` (side nav or tab bar), `main` (tiles), `aside` labelled by the panel heading (desktop/tablet).
- **No charts** in v1; rows and tiles only. No animation over 200 ms; no slide-in for the panel under reduced motion.
- **Reflow:** works at 320 CSS px and 400% zoom; long Bangla labels and lakh figures wrap rather than clip; row amounts never truncate (`white-space: nowrap` on the amount only, label wraps).
- **Language:** `lang="bn"` or `lang="en"` on the page; Product, Warehouse and person names are not marked or translated.
- **Performance:** first figures within NFR-1 targets; show cached figures with "as of" while refreshing.

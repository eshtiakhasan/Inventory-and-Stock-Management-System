## Overview

**Page Purpose:** List the Products whose Promisable quantity at a Warehouse is at or below that Warehouse's Low-stock threshold (DD-3), with the quantity already on open POs, out-of-stock (Promisable 0) first (FR-50), so the reader can tell what to reorder.

**User Situation:** Mr. Haque has just assigned the unexplained case to the Manager on Issue detail and comes back to check what is running low before Monday's supplier calls. He wants a short list he can act on, not a report. The same page serves every Role (a Supervisor or Sales rep checking from More → Low stock on a phone).

**Success Criteria:**
- He sees which Products are low at which Warehouse, and which of them already have stock coming on an open PO, without opening any Product.
- He can pick out the two Products with nothing on order (the ones to reorder) in under a minute.
- Out-of-stock lines are first and are marked by text and icon, not color alone.

**Entry Points:**
- Low stock tile on [04.1 Owner view](../04.1-owner-view/04.1-owner-view.md) (Owner, Manager, Accountant).
- More → Low stock (phone, every Role; EXPERIENCE.md → Home and navigation per Role).

**Exit Points:**
- A row → Product stock (S6, [07.2 Product stock](../../07-tanvir-promises-stock-while-the-customer-waits/07.2-product-stock/07.2-product-stock.md)) for that Product, that Warehouse's ladder first.
- Back → Owner view (or More).
- Owner / Manager only: "Set low-stock thresholds" → [09.4 Settings: Products](../../09-haque-sets-up-his-business/09.4-settings-products/09.4-settings-products.md) (empty states only).
- Scenario end: notes the two Products to reorder for Monday.

## Layout Structure

One list, grouped by Warehouse. Every Role sees the Warehouses it can see stock for; the User's own Warehouse group comes first (as the stock ladder does), then the others in name order. Above the list: a Warehouse filter (chips). Within a group: out-of-stock lines first (FR-50), then the rest by how far below the threshold they are (lowest Promisable as a share of threshold first) [new assumption, Open Question 4].

- **Phone (320–599px):** app bar with Back; single column; each line is a 64px+ row card with Product name, Promisable, threshold and open-PO quantity on separate lines; bottom tab bar (the tab that led here stays active: Overview from the tile, More from More).
- **Tablet / desktop (≥ 600px):** content in a table per Warehouse group: Product | Promisable | Threshold | On open POs. Desktop keeps the side nav with "Overview" current (this page is a child of the Owner view; S19 has no side-nav item). For Roles without Overview, the side nav marks nothing and the page is reached from More (phone) only.

## Technical Notes

- **Rule (FR-50, DD-3):** a line is low when Promisable at a Warehouse ≤ that Warehouse's Low-stock threshold for the Product (threshold set per Warehouse, FR-6). Out of stock means Promisable = 0; those lines come first. Quantity on open POs = ordered minus received on POs that are Sent or Partly received for that Warehouse.
- **Count consistency:** the number of lines here (all Warehouses, no filter) equals the Owner view Low stock tile count (04.1), except for a User assigned to an open count, whose list leaves out the counted Products (DD-7); if that User also sees the tile, the tile excludes them too [ASSUMPTION].
- **Count blinding (DD-7):** while a count is open, the server omits low-stock lines for its Products at its Warehouse from the payload for Users assigned to count it, and from the offline cache on their phone. The Manager who started the count is not blinded unless also assigned to count.
- **Visibility:** all Roles (FR-4). Warehouses shown = those the User can see stock for; the User's own Warehouse group first.
- **Units:** show quantities in Pack and Base units ("4 ctn + 6 pcs"); thresholds are stored in Base units and displayed the same way.
- **Accessibility:** one H1, H2 per Warehouse group; desktop uses a real `table` with `th scope="col"` and the Product name as the only link in each row; phone row cards are single links with a full accessible name. Out-of-stock and low status are text + icon, never color alone. Works at 320 CSS px and 400% zoom; long Bangla labels wrap.
- **No sorting controls or charts** in v1; fixed sort as above. No infinite scroll.

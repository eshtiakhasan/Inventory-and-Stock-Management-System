## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let a counter count the Products assigned to him **without seeing any system quantity**: scan a Product, type or step the count, Save line. Each saved line is recorded under his name with the quantity and the time, and **moves no stock**; everything is compared and posted when the Manager closes the count (FR-35, DD-6, the count exception in DD-1).

**User Situation:** Karim, Warehouse worker at Narayanganj, Saturday morning. Rafiq has started the weekly count of the rice and oil aisle; Karim's phone shows "Stock count SC-015 · 10 lines" in My tasks. He walks the aisle with the shared phone, scanning and counting stacks. Signal in the back aisle comes and goes. He wants his counts kept even offline, and he does not want to be blamed for a number he cannot check (Karim persona: fear of blame, of lost work).

**Success Criteria:**
- He counts a line with scan + count + one tap on Save line (the blind-screen exception to NFR-2).
- No On hand, expected, previous count, other counter's count or difference appears anywhere: on screen, after save, in `aria-*`, hidden text or page code (State Patterns → Blind; FR-35). Until Close count, every other screen (Product search, Product stock, Low stock, scan results) shows "Hidden during count" for the Products in this count at this Warehouse (DD-7).
- Each save shows "Saved" (online) or "Saved on phone, will sync" (offline) and the line shows "Counted {qty}" (his own number only).
- After his last line he is back in My tasks with the next task (FR-53); whether his lines matched is never shown to him here; mismatches become Discrepancy cases decided by someone who did not count them (FR-36, FR-37, FR-5).

**Entry Points:**
- Stock count task card on [01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md) (`mytasks-card-count`).
- Back from [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md) (Close, no change) or after a scan (line selected).

**Exit Points:**
- Scan bar → [01.3 Scan overlay](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md) in blind count context.
- Saving his **last uncounted line** → [01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md) with toast "Count lines saved · SC-015" and the next task (as 01.1 lists: "last count line (08.2)").
- Back (app bar) → My tasks; saved lines stay saved (recorded, no stock moved yet, DD-6); the card shows "In progress".
- Offline banner → [01.4 Offline queue](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).
- Scenario continues on the Manager's desk: [08.1 Stock counts](../08.1-stock-counts/08.1-stock-counts.md) → Close count → cases in [03.2](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md).

## Technical Notes

- **Blind is a data rule (FR-35, State Patterns → Blind):** the S11 payload, offline cache, `aria-*`, hidden text and analytics hold no On hand, no expected or previous quantity, no other counter's numbers, no comparison result. Type code results in this context show no stock figures. **DD-7:** beyond this screen, the server withholds On hand, Promisable, the ladder, movement quantities and low status for this count's Products at this Warehouse from every assigned counter on every screen ("Hidden during count") until Close count.
- **Stock posting (DD-6, the count exception in DD-1):** each Save line records the count line (User, quantity, device time, Warehouse; server time added on sync, FR-43) and **moves no stock**. Close count on 08.1 compares each line with On hand as of its counted time, opens Discrepancy cases and moves shortages to Unexplained. A line that arrives after close never changes the closed count; it opens a Late count line Issue (DD-6).
- **Action IDs (FR-14):** every Save line carries a device-generated unique ID; double taps and offline retries apply once.
- **Offline (FR-41):** Save line queues; the line list and catalogue are on the phone; progress is computed on the phone.
- **Units (FR-9):** stored in Base units; shown as Pack + leftover Base.
- **Live regions:** toast and scan match to polite; server refusals and "count closed" once each.
- **Focus:** after scan → the focused unit field; after save → the Scan bar; after the last line → My tasks' Scan bar.
- **Who / when / where** on every line (SM-3).

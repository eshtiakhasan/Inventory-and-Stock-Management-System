## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let a Warehouse worker pick a Dispatch order line by line with big picked / ordered progress, recording each line in one scan plus one tap (each confirmed line is its own Stock movement out of the Business, DD-1), and then confirm the dispatch to close the order (FR-25).

**User Situation:** Karim, mid-afternoon, picking DO-1042 (a rice-heavy order for Rahman Traders) at Narayanganj. The customer's van leaves in 30 minutes. He holds the shared phone in one hand and cartons with the other. He wants the order complete and on time; he worries that recording a crushed carton will cost him paperwork or blame (scenario Q3, Q4; [Karim persona](../../../B-Trigger-Map/personas/02-Karim-the-Warehouse-Worker.md)).

**Success Criteria:**
- Picking a line costs **1 scan + 1 tap** (Confirm line) with the pre-filled quantity accepted; ticking a line without scanning costs **1 tap** (NFR-2, FR-25).
- After each Confirm line the line shows its new progress ("20 / 20 ctn") and status, the toast confirms, and focus returns to the Scan bar (EXPERIENCE.md → Floor primary button).
- The Flag damage button is always one tap away in the fixed bottom zone; flagging never changes the line's picked count when a replacement can be reserved (FR-29, see [02.2](../02.2-flag-damage/02.2-flag-damage.md)).
- Confirm dispatch is available at any time; a dialog appears only when lines are short (EXPERIENCE.md → Line completion).
- Everything works offline and queues (FR-41, FR-42); the screen always shows online or offline.

**Entry Points:**
- Tap on the Dispatch order task card in [My tasks (01.1)](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md) (FR-53).
- Back from the Scan overlay (Close) or from the Flag damage sheet returns here with the scroll position kept.

**Exit Points:**
- **Flag damage** button → Scan overlay in Flag damage mode ([02.2](../02.2-flag-damage/02.2-flag-damage.md)); returns here after the scan.
- **Scan bar** → base Scan overlay ([01.3](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.3-scan-overlay/01.3-scan-overlay.md)); returns here with the scanned line selected.
- **Confirm dispatch** → My tasks with the next task on screen (FR-25, FR-53).
- **Back** (app bar) → My tasks, nothing changed; lines already confirmed stay saved (their stock has already moved, DD-1).
- **Offline banner** → [Offline queue (01.4)](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).

## Layout Structure

Single column at 360 CSS px. Sticky app bar on top, optional offline banner under it, a scrolling list of order lines with the current line expanded to its stacked quantity entry and Confirm line button, then Confirm dispatch at the end of the list. A fixed bottom zone holds the Flag damage button above the Scan bar (DESIGN.md → Layout & Spacing). The toast floats above the fixed zone.

**Short screen (< 30rem tall), landscape or text ≥ 150%:** only the Scan bar stays fixed at `{spacing.scan-bar-height-compact}`; the Flag damage button moves to the end of the content, after Confirm dispatch; the app bar compacts to name + online status and the title scrolls with content. **Wide phones and tablets (≥ 600px):** same single column; the quantity entry's carton and pieces rows may sit side by side (DESIGN.md → Quantity entry). No desktop layout.

## Technical Notes

- **Recording model (DD-1):** each Confirm line is its own Stock movement Reserved → out of the Business ("dispatched"), queued offline like any action. Confirm dispatch closes the order and raises discrepancy Issues for short lines; it posts no movement for lines already confirmed. Partial dispatch allowed; the rest stays Reserved.
- **Duplicate-safe:** every Confirm line, Confirm dispatch and flag carries a device-created unique action ID; buttons disable on tap (FR-14).
- **Offline:** order lines, Product catalogue with Barcodes and Conversion factors, and last-known stock are on the phone. Picks and dispatch confirmation queue (FR-41) and sync in order (FR-43); refusals become Sync conflicts (FR-44).
- **Quantities** stored in Base units, shown as "3 ctn + 5 pcs" (FR-9). Western digits, `tabular-nums`.
- **Speed:** saving < 2 s on a low-cost Android over 3G/4G (NFR-1); spinner only inside the button while saving.
- **Accessibility:** one polite and one assertive live region from page load; bilingual buttons get their name from visible `lang`-marked text, no `aria-label`; `scroll-padding` keeps the focused element above the fixed zone; focus ring two-tone, never `primary`; works at 320 CSS px, 400% zoom and 200% text; orientation not locked.
- **Shared phone:** signed-in name always in the app bar.
- **Audit:** every movement holds User, device and server time, Warehouse, Reason and a link to the Dispatch order (FR-11).

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let Karim report a damaged carton the moment he finds it, in **one tap (Flag damage) plus one scan**, with the scan itself saving the flag: 1 of the scanned unit, Reason "damaged", from Reserved on the order he is picking so a replacement is reserved automatically (FR-29, NFR-2). Corrections and the no-camera case go through one bottom sheet.

**User Situation:** On the second line of DO-1042 Karim finds a Soybean oil carton crushed at the corner. The van leaves in 30 minutes. His worry: that reporting it costs five minutes of paperwork, or that the damage lands on him (scenario Q4). The flag records who, when and where (FR-11), which becomes his proof in [My record](../../06-karim-proves-where-a-carton-went/06.1-my-record/06.1-my-record.md).

**Success Criteria:**
- Default path is exactly **1 tap + 1 scan**; no form, no dialog (EXPERIENCE.md → Budget; Confirm vs undo).
- Flag damage mode can never be mistaken for a normal scan (red strip, warning icon, its own dialog name).
- After the scan Karim is back on the pick with the line's picked count **unchanged** (re-reservation), sees "Flagged 1 ctn damaged", and can fix quantity or Reason from the toast (UJ-2).
- Works without a camera and offline (FR-39, FR-41).
- NFR-11: a new worker completes a Damage flag after a 10-minute demo, no manual.

**Entry Points:**
- **Flag damage** button in the fixed zone of [02.1 Pick Dispatch order](../02.1-pick-dispatch-order/02.1-pick-dispatch-order.md) (S8). Also from Product stock (S6), where the flag is taken from Available (not part of this scenario).
- **Change qty/reason** in the flag toast, or on the flag in [My record (06.1)](../../06-karim-proves-where-a-carton-went/06.1-my-record/06.1-my-record.md) until the flag is decided.

**Exit Points:**
- Scan saved → back on 02.1, current line kept, toast shown, focus on the Scan bar. Karim continues picking and later taps **Confirm dispatch** (scenario end).
- **Close** (overlay or sheet), Escape or Back → back on 02.1, nothing saved, focus returns to the Flag damage button (or the toast action that opened the sheet; if the toast is gone, the Scan bar).
- Sheet saved → back on 02.1 with the flag toast.

## Technical Notes

- **Scan saves only in this mode** (EXPERIENCE.md → When does a scan record). The overlay must carry the mode explicitly so a normal scan can never save a flag; the mode ends after one read, Close, or leaving the page.
- **Unit from Barcode:** Pack barcode → 1 carton; Base barcode → 1 piece (FR-29 "1 of the scanned unit"). Stored in Base units (FR-9).
- **Stock movements (FR-11, FR-29):** from S8 with the Product on the order: Reserved → Damaged for that order, then Available → Reserved for the same order (re-reservation); short if Available is insufficient, raising a Dispatch-short Issue. Otherwise Available → Damaged. Every movement holds User, device and server time, Warehouse, Reason "damaged" and a link to the Damage flag and Dispatch order. A Damage flag Issue goes to the inbox (FR-30).
- **Corrections are append-only:** a linked correction, never an edit (FR-11). Change qty/reason is offered until the flag is decided.
- **Duplicate-safe:** each flag and correction carries a device-created action ID (FR-14); the camera closes after one read; buttons disable on tap.
- **Offline:** flags and corrections queue (FR-41) and sync in order (FR-43); server refusals become Sync conflicts (FR-44), never silently dropped.
- **No photo** in v1 (FR-29: photo upload is SHOULD).
- **Accessibility:** overlay and sheet are dialogs with focus trap and return; bilingual save button named by visible `lang`-marked text; announcements via the shared polite/assertive regions; no swipe-to-dismiss on the sheet (Tap to act); vibration plus text, never vibration alone.

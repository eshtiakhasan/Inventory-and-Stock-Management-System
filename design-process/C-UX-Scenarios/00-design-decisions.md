# Cross-Cutting Design Decisions: Mojud

> Decisions that apply to every page spec. Where a page spec, mockup,
> EXPERIENCE.md, DESIGN.md or the PRD disagrees with this file, **this file wins**.
> Items marked `[ASSUMPTION]` still need validating with real distributors or in
> architecture.

| ID | Date | Decision | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| DD-1 | 2026-10-23, amended 2026-10-24 | **Stock moves on each saved line**, with two exceptions. On Receive PO, Receive Transfer and Pick (Confirm line), every saved line is its own Stock movement, queued offline like any action. The finish buttons (Mark delivery complete, Finish receipt, Confirm dispatch) close the task and raise shortfall or difference Issues; they never post the lines again. **Exceptions:** Send Transfer lines (DD-5) and Count lines (DD-6) are recorded per line but move no stock until the finish action. | 01.2, 02.1, 05.2, 05.3, 08.1, 08.2, 01.4 |
| DD-2 | 2026-10-23 | **Issues are ranked by ৳, but amounts are shown by role.** The inbox still ranks by value at risk (FR-31). Taka amounts appear only to roles allowed to see Stock value (Owner, Manager, Accountant, per FR-4). Supervisors and Sales reps see quantity and unit instead, never ৳. | 03.1, 03.2, 06.1, any card that shows value |
| DD-3 | 2026-10-23 | **Low stock compares Promisable, at or below the threshold.** A product is low when Promisable ≤ threshold, per warehouse. Out of stock means Promisable = 0. | 04.1 (low-stock tile), 04.2, 07.1, 07.2 |
| DD-4 | 2026-10-23 | **The fixed Scan bar appears on floor screens only.** That means My tasks and the task screens (S3, S6–S11) for floor roles. Desk-role home screens (Issues inbox, Owner view, Product search for the Sales rep) have no fixed Scan bar; scanning goes through the Scan tab, or the search field's scan button where a role has no Scan tab. | All pages |
| DD-5 | 2026-10-24 | **A transfer is a draft until it is sent.** Saved Send Transfer lines are a **draft**: the stock stays Available in the source warehouse, and nothing is reserved. Tapping **Send Transfer** posts all lines to In transit in one movement per line, at that moment. If Available has dropped below a draft line by then, Send is refused for that line with the maximum ("Only 18 ctn available. Max 18 ctn."). Drafts are always visible: a "Draft transfer · not sent" card in the creator's My tasks, and a **Drafts** segment in Transfers. The creator, a Supervisor, a Manager or the Owner can **Discard draft**. The Issue's decisions are **Keep draft** (check again in 24 h) and **Discard draft** `[ASSUMPTION]`. A draft not sent within **24 h** raises a **Draft transfer not sent** Issue to the source Supervisor `[ASSUMPTION: 24 h]`. Once a transfer is sent, the existing 48 h Transfer-overdue Issue (FR-28) applies, so stock never sits In transit without someone being told. | 01.1, 03.2, 05.1, 05.2, 06.1, 01.4 |
| DD-6 | 2026-10-24 | **Count discrepancies apply when the count is closed.** Each saved count line is recorded (counter, quantity, time) but moves no stock. At **Close count**, each line is compared with On hand *as of the moment it was counted* (FR-36 rule kept); mismatches open Discrepancy cases, and shortages move to Unexplained then. A count line that arrives **after** the count is closed (an offline sync) never changes the closed count. It opens a **Late count line** Issue for the Manager who started the count, showing counted vs On hand and the times, with the decisions **Start recount** or **Dismiss** `[ASSUMPTION: two decisions are enough]`. Close count warns, in its confirm dialog, if any assigned counter still has lines not synced to the server ("2 lines from Karim not received yet"). | 08.1, 08.2, 03.2, 01.4, 04.1, 06.1 |
| DD-7 | 2026-10-24 | **Counters can't see system quantities during a blind count.** While a count is open, a User assigned to count it cannot see On hand, Promisable, the stock ladder, movement quantities or low-stock status **for the Products in that count, at that Warehouse**, on any screen (Product search, Product stock, Low stock, scan results). They see "Hidden during count" instead. Everything else stays normal, and the restriction ends at Close count. The Manager who started the count sees progress (lines counted) but no counted quantities until Close. The server enforces this; the UI does not just hide it. | 07.1, 07.2, 04.2, 08.1, 08.2, 01.3 |
| DD-8 | 2026-10-24 | **Shared phones don't save passwords, and queued actions stay with their author.** (a) Each device has a **Shared phone** setting, asked at the first sign-in on that device and on by default for floor roles `[ASSUMPTION: default]`. When it is on, the sign-in form asks the browser not to save the password (`autocomplete="off"` on the password field, no "remember me") and shows a short hint not to save the password. Browsers may still offer to save it `[ASSUMPTION: test on Chrome Android]`. Owners on personal devices keep password-manager support (09.1). (b) Every queued action is stamped at recording time with its author and a signed per-session action token. On sign-out with unsynced actions, a dialog says "3 actions not synced yet. They stay on this phone and will sync as Karim." Those actions keep syncing as their author even after sign-out. A different User who signs in can't edit, discard or re-send them, and sees only a count ("2 actions from another person waiting to sync"). New actions are always stamped with the current User `[ASSUMPTION: signed action tokens to be confirmed in architecture]`. | 10.1, 10.3, 01.4, 01.1 |
| DD-9 | 2026-10-24 | **Owners reach Orders from the phone tab bar.** The Owner's phone tabs become **Overview · Issues · Stock · Orders · More**. The Manager has no Orders tab, so **Orders** is the first item in More for the Manager `[ASSUMPTION: five tabs fit at 320 px with Bangla labels; verify]`. Orders keeps its segments (Dispatch orders, Purchase orders, Transfers) filtered by FR-4. | 04.1, 03.1, 05.1, 07.3, 09.7 |
| DD-10 | 2026-10-24 | **Products have alternate names that search matches.** Each Product has its main name (shown everywhere, exactly as entered, FR-45) plus optional **Alternate names** in either language, e.g. "চাল 25 কেজি" for "Rice 25 kg" `[ASSUMPTION: up to 5 per Product]`. Product search matches the main name, alternate names, SKU and Barcode. When the match came from an alternate name, the result shows it under the main name. There is no automatic transliteration. Alternate names are edited in Settings: Products and imported through a new optional **Alternate names** column in the Products Excel template (separated by `;`). | 07.1, 09.3, 09.4, 01.3 |

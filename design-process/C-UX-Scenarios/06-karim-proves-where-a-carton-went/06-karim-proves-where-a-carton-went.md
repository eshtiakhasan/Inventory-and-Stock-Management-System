**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Show, from his own record, that a missing carton went missing after his handoff.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Floor adoption
**Objective:** Objective 2 (SM-2): recording pays the worker back, which keeps them recording

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** [Karim the Warehouse Worker](../../B-Trigger-Map/personas/02-Karim-the-Warehouse-Worker.md) (Primary)
**Situation:** Karim, Thursday morning, when the Manager asks him about a missing carton of Rice 25 kg from Monday's transfer.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Clear his name quickly without an argument.

**Worry:** Being blamed because nobody can prove otherwise.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile
**Entry:** Opens More → My record on the warehouse phone while the Manager is standing next to him.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
His record shows he sent 20 ctn at 10:12 and Gazipur received 19 at 16:40; he hands the phone to the Manager and is cleared.

**Business Success:**
Honest worker protected by the same record that holds people accountable, which builds floor trust and adoption.

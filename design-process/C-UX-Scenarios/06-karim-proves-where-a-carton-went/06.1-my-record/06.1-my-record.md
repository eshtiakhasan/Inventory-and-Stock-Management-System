## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show a User, in plain counts, what they recorded today, this week or this month, and list the **cases linked to their own actions** (Transfer Issues, Discrepancy cases, Sync conflicts, Damage flags) **with the outcome of each**, so the record that holds people accountable also protects them (FR-51).

**User Situation:** Karim, Thursday morning at Narayanganj. The Manager is standing next to him asking about a missing carton of Rice 25 kg from Monday's Transfer to Gazipur. Karim opens More → My record. He hopes to clear his name quickly without an argument; he worries he will be blamed because nobody can prove otherwise.

**Success Criteria:**
- In two taps (This week, then the linked row) Karim shows: sent 20 ctn by Karim at Mon 10:12; received 19 ctn by Jamal at Gazipur at Mon 16:40; 1 ctn still In transit, linked to the Transfer (FR-28; UJ-9 steps 3–4).
- Every count is in plain words and numbers, no charts, no ৳ (DD-2 for floor roles).
- Outcomes are stated the same way whether or not the gap was at his step: no blame words (Voice and Tone; UJ-9 failure path).
- Actions not yet synced show "Not synced"; refused ones show "Not applied – sent to supervisor" with the reason (FR-44).
- Offline, the page still opens with the last loaded record labelled "as of HH:MM (offline)" (FR-42).

**Entry Points:**
- More → My record (all Roles; scenario path).
- "My record" link in the refused-item sheet of [01.4 Offline queue](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).
- Empty My tasks summary on [01.1 My tasks](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.1-my-tasks/01.1-my-tasks.md) ("All tasks done for today" summary comes from here).
- Owner / Manager: a User in [09.6 Settings: Users](../../09-haque-sets-up-his-business/09.6-settings-users/09.6-settings-users.md) ("View record") or the person picker on this page (Open Question 4).

**Exit Points:**
- Linked case row → [03.2 Issue detail](../../03-nasima-clears-the-inbox/03.2-issue-detail/03.2-issue-detail.md) in **View only** (or Decided) state, with both counts, both Users and both times (scenario climax).
- "Not synced" section / refused chip → [01.4 Offline queue](../../01-karim-receives-a-delivery-blind/01.4-offline-queue/01.4-offline-queue.md).
- Damage flag row, still open → "Change qty/reason" sheet of [02.2 Flag damage](../../02-karim-picks-and-flags-damage/02.2-flag-damage/02.2-flag-damage.md) (Component Patterns → Flag damage sheet [ASSUMPTION]).
- Back → More, scroll kept.

## Layout Structure

A read-mostly list page under More. Sticky app bar, optional offline banner, (Owner / Manager) person picker, period chips with the "as of" line, an optional **Not synced** block, the **Summary** card of plain counts, **Cases linked to my actions**, then **My actions** grouped by day. Per DD-4 there is **no fixed Scan bar**; the fixed bottom zone holds the bottom tab bar with **More** active.

## Technical Notes

- **Source of truth:** counts and cases come from the server's movement and Issue records (User, time, Warehouse on every movement; SM-3). The phone merges in its own queued actions (Not synced block) and never counts them in the Summary until accepted.
- **Offline cache:** the last loaded record for the signed-in User (each period loaded) is kept on the phone [ASSUMPTION: not listed in EXPERIENCE.md → Foundation → Offline data; Open Question 5]. Other Users' records are never cached.
- **Blind safety:** this page never shows the expected / sent / On hand quantity for a line the User counted blind while its Issue is open (Open Question 1). The read-only Issue detail it links to must apply the same guard.
- **No ৳:** no Stock value or value at risk on this page for any Role (DD-2 for floor roles; v1 choice for desk roles).
- **Linking:** each Issue keeps the ID of the movement(s) that caused it, so "linked to my actions" is a server query, not a guess. Transfer Issues link to both the sender's and the receiver's movements.
- **Shared phones:** the page always shows whose record it is (title) and who is signed in (app bar); signing out (Settings: Me) clears the cached record [ASSUMPTION].
- **Focus:** on arrival, the `h1`; after a period change, stays on the chip; Back from 03.2 restores scroll and focus to the opened row.
